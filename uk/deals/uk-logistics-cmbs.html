Commercial implications Analysis

The £603m participation is approximately 80.1% of the £753m loan (603/753); this is not loan-to-value, nor a separate £603m acquisition. A short floating-rate loan against longer-life properties introduces extension and refinancing risk. The note waterfall, collateral coverage and servicing arrangements govern how underlying borrower risk reaches investors.
