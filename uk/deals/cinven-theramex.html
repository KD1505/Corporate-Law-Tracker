Acquisition and business footprint

Documented finding

Cinven reached an agreement with Carlyle and PAI Partners for Theramex. The company commercialises treatments across menopause, osteoporosis, contraception and fertility. Its London headquarters and presence across many markets make the acquisition relevant to UK and cross-border legal work. Financial details are undisclosed by the buyer.

Product IP and licensing

Question: Does the business own or in-license each product and in which territories?

Value depends on enforceable territorial rights, royalties, duration, exclusivity, change-of-control consents and termination rights.

Documents to examine: Product and licence schedules; material agreements.

Medicines regulation

Question: Which marketing authorisations, regulatory filings and pharmacovigilance obligations need continuity?

Separate legal entity control from transfer of individual authorisations. Assess regulator notifications and product-safety responsibilities country by country.

Documents to examine: Authorisation register and safety systems; local law advice.

Sponsor exit

Question: What historic acquisition obligations, management incentives and leakage risks survive the sale?

Review legacy indemnities, deferred consideration, management reinvestment, W&I policy scope and limitations. No assumption that any specific device was used.

Documents to examine: SPA, prior acquisition documents, insurance policies (not reviewed).

Leveraged finance

Question: How would financing diligence address pharma revenue durability and IP risks?

Cash flows can be sensitive to patent/licence expiry, reimbursement, supply disruptions and product liability. Link debt availability to actual closing conditions.

Documents to examine: Facility documents and financial model (not reviewed).
