Commercial implications Analysis

Project economics depend on construction cost, revenue arrangements, grid connection and risk allocation. The investment decision does not eliminate construction risk; advisers must distinguish committed funding, drawdown conditions and completion milestones.

What the lawyers may work on Illustrative

Map procurement, construction, grid and offtake interfaces; consider security, lender step-in and completion support. Model cost overruns and delays rather than assuming investment approval proves funding has been drawn.

Next milestone

Construction, grid connection and financing milestones; distinguish investment approval from operational completion.

Ownership and project economics

Documented finding

KKR acquired 50% of each Vanguard project, with both sponsors committing equity. The approximately £12bn budget relates to development of the two projects, not the price KKR paid for the stakes.

  • RWE Equity close
  • KKR Joint announcement

Financing components

Documented finding

Financial close includes £11.1bn cash facilities, £1.7bn ancillary facilities and participation by 35 banks. The £1bn NWF commitment is a component of the package, not an additional transaction valuation. Euler Hermes and EIFO provide credit cover.

  • RWE Financing package
  • KKR Shared announcement

Revenue support

Documented finding

Official AR7 results identify both projects at £91.20/MWh in 2024 prices. The sponsors report 20-year indexed CfDs and expect full commissioning in 2030. These are not guaranteed profitability or completed capacity.

  • DESNZ Vanguard East and West results
  • RWE CfD and construction schedule

CfD economics

Question: Which revenue risks persist with a CfD?

Model generation volumes, reference prices, negative pricing, availability and expiry.

Documents to examine: Executed CfDs; AR7 terms; grid agreements.

JV governance

Question: How does equal ownership manage funding calls?

Check reserved matters, deadlock, default funding, transfers and exit rights.

Documents to examine: Shareholders agreements; equity commitments.

Construction
