Commercial implications Analysis

A pension risk-transfer acquisition combines long-duration liabilities and investment portfolios. The buyer's capacity to source suitable assets and maintain prudential capital matters alongside purchase price. Integration and group supervision are separate from completion.

What the lawyers may work on Illustrative

Assess change-in-control notifications, insurance solvency and group structure, investment and reinsurance arrangements, funding conditions, warranties and post-closing governance.

Next milestone

The separately proposed UK headquarters relocation and applicable supervisory arrangements.
