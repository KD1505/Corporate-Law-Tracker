Perimeter and consideration

Documented finding

The proposed acquisition covers all shares in MetLife UK Limited, which stopped writing new business in 2017. It excludes the seller's ongoing UK protection business. The price is not quantified; Chesnara describes the consideration as subject to closing adjustments and not expected to be material.

Portfolio economics

Documented finding

The issuer records approximately 18,000 customers and £1.7bn of assets under administration at 31 December 2025. Slaughter and May also reports the portfolio scale. The assets administered are not the purchase price or the buyer's equity value.

  • Chesnara Portfolio background

Forecast and operating model

Forecast / announced expectation

Chesnara estimates £90m lifetime cash flow and identifies existing administration by SS&C. Its announcement states that an external reinsurance arrangement for variable-annuity guarantee risk will remain in place. The cash-flow projection is not cash received at closing; service continuity and recoverability matter to its realisation.

Practical legal workstreams

Original analyst diligence and execution questions. These are not a claim that particular clauses were agreed or that a firm performed these tasks.

Prudential diligence

Question: What liabilities, own-funds movements and solvency sensitivities sit behind expected cash flows?

A portfolio of policies may have guarantees and future liability run-off. Assess stress scenarios, reserving, asset-liability matching and regulatory capital, not just AUA.

Documents to examine: Actuarial reports, solvency disclosures, price-adjustment mechanism.

Reinsurance

Question: Will the guarantee-risk protection survive ownership change?

Review counterparty credit, exclusions, termination, collateral and consent conditions; an external arrangement is not risk elimination.

Documents to examine: Reinsurance treaty and counterparty due diligence.

Outsourcing

Question: How do SS&C's service arrangements support or limit the operating model?

Examine service levels, exit/transition rights, operational resilience, subcontracting and data migration. Shared provider does not mean automatic contract continuity.

Documents to examine: Administration agreement and service-level schedules.

Closing

Question: How should the purchase-price adjustment and regulatory approvals be tracked?

Separate price, portfolio value and expected future capital emergence; ensure the precise control approvals and conditions are evidenced before announcing completion.

Documents to examine: SPA and regulatory submissions (not reviewed).
