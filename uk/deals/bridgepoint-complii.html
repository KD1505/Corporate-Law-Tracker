Commercial implications Analysis

This is a sponsor-backed control acquisition in a compliance-driven services sector. Recurring demand is an investment hypothesis, not established profitability. Test revenue quality, accreditation, liability exposures and integration capability before assessing the valuation.

Operational diligence

Question: Which certifications and customer contracts are material?

Verify accreditation, renewal conditions, change-of-control provisions and historic liabilities.

Documents to examine: Accreditation register; contracts; insurance

Financing and valuation

Question: How resilient are earnings and cash conversion?

Assess recurring versus project revenue and downside funding rather than assuming a disclosed leverage level.

Documents to examine: Financial diligence report; financing commitments

Risk allocation
