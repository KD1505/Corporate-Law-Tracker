CIP disclosed its investment decision on 2 October for the 350MW, four-hour Scottish storage project. Noriker's counsel disclosed on 28 October that Noriker had sold the project in January 2026 and continued supporting construction. The announced project commitment is not the sale price.

Commercial implications Analysis

Battery storage economics depend on grid access, availability and revenue arrangements. A developer selling a project but retaining construction responsibilities faces distinct execution obligations; the investment commitment should not be treated as seller consideration.

What the lawyers may work on Illustrative

Diligence land rights, planning and grid arrangements; allocate construction and performance risk; review the construction management agreement, procurement and revenue contract.
