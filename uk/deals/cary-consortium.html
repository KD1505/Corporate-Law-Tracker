Commercial implications Analysis

A consortium structure raises governance questions distinct from the purchase price: reserved matters, future funding, exit rights and alignment between investors with different investment horizons.

What the lawyers may work on Illustrative

Set governance, reserved matters, information and transfer rights; specify funding commitments and exit process. Diligence multi-country operating subsidiaries and local approvals.

Next milestone

Regulatory approvals and confirmation of closing; the announced expectation was the first half of 2027.
