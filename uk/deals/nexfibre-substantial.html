Commercial implications Analysis

Wholesale network ownership and the separate retail transfer create different commercial and regulatory workstreams. The parties' claimed scale and investment benefits must be tested against the CMA's provisional concerns about independent wholesale alternatives; funding commitments are not the purchase price.
