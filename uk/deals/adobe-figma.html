Adobe and Figma agreed to terminate their merger agreement on 18 December 2023 after concluding that required regulatory approvals lacked a clear path. The CMA cancelled its inquiry. No acquisition completed.

Commercial implications Analysis

The case illustrates execution risk in acquisitions of innovative competitors. Even a signed agreement can fail if approvals cannot be obtained. Deal costs, opportunity costs and termination provisions matter to the parties' expected return.

What the lawyers may work on Illustrative

Negotiate regulatory efforts, termination rights, outside dates and any reverse break fee; preserve independent operation while closing remains conditional. Model the buyer's exposure if the acquisition is abandoned.
