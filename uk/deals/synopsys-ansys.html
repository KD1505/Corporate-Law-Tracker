Commercial implications Analysis

Engineering design and simulation are complementary across the chip-to-system workflow, but the divestitures show that complementarity at portfolio level can coexist with rivalry in individual software products. The original EV is tied to the December 2023 stock price; compare it with closing accounting only after reconciling the different basis and stock consideration.

What the lawyers may work on Illustrative

Diligence software licensing, IP and customer concentration; separate remedy assets and ongoing services; coordinate cross-border conditions and transitional arrangements.

Offer economics

Documented finding

The announced $197 cash plus 0.345 buyer shares produced an implied $390.19 per target share on the 21 December 2023 reference price. Enterprise value was approximately $35bn; the buyer's later accounting shows $34.9bn consideration.

Financing and accounting

Documented finding

The original $16bn committed debt was distinct from consideration. Closing accounting records $17.6bn cash, $17.1bn stock fair value and a balance relating to awards and pre-existing relationships.

  • Synopsys and Ansys Transaction terms, advisers and stated synergies

Regulatory perimeter

Documented finding
