Commercial implications Analysis

The combination expands convenience-food capabilities, but the CMA concern was focused on a narrower product market. Evaluate divestment separately from forecast integration gains. Use combined financial reporting rather than assuming synergies have been realised.

What the lawyers may work on Illustrative

Define remedy perimeter, transition arrangements and customer continuity; allocate responsibility for regulatory conditions and post-closing commitments.
