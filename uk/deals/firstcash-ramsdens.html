Original offer versus revised price

Documented finding

The initial bid valued Ramsdens at about £206m fully diluted including the permitted dividend entitlement. The July revision increased the cash price from 600p to 675p. Including up to 9p dividends, total shareholder value is up to 684p or approximately £232m fully diluted. The cash consideration itself is approximately £229m. This is a proposed acquisition, not a completed payment.

  • Gowling WLG Initial mandate and valuation
  • FirstCash / Ramsdens Revised offer and Appendix II

Premium and value bridge

Documented finding

The revised announcement calculates a 49% premium for the 675p cash price to the 453p close on 22 June; including the permitted dividends it reports 51%. Its pre-IFRS 16 enterprise valuation is about £229m, distinct from the coincidentally similar aggregate cash payment. The disclosed net cash adjustment is measured at 31 March.

  • FirstCash / Ramsdens Premiums and Appendix II valuation methodology

Financing evolution

Documented finding

The July announcement describes a bridge credit agreement amended to provide up to £239m. A subsequent announcement envisages funding from FirstCash's unsecured US revolving facility and refers to a tenth amendment announced 31 August. It also anticipates fees and possible repayment of target indebtedness. The final funding mix or amount drawn has not been established.

  • FirstCash / Ramsdens Financing of the acquisition
  • FirstCash / Bidco FirstCash RCF Agreement

Regulatory status

Documented finding

The CMA opened the formal inquiry on 26 October; its published Phase 1 deadline is 22 December subject to any extension. A scheme recommendation is not evidence that competition conditions have been satisfied.

  • CMA Statutory timetable and Phase 1

Adviser allocation

Documented finding

The Rule 2.7 announcement identifies Alston & Bird and Gowling WLG as US and English law advisers to the bidder side, and Addleshaw Goddard for Ramsdens. Gowling's separate client announcement confirms its Chess Bidco role. The original mandate publication's valuation predates the higher offer.

Practical legal workstreams

Original analyst diligence and execution questions. These are not a claim that particular clauses were agreed or that a firm performed these tasks.

Public takeover

Question: How do permitted dividends and a final offer statement affect the price?

Model dividend payment timing, reductions for other distributions and the expressly reserved exceptions to the final offer; track the scheme conditions rather than extrapolate a new bid.

Documents to examine: Rule 2.7 and revised announcement; scheme document; Takeover Code.

Competition

Question: Where might local overlap arise in a consumer financial-services acquisition?

Assess customer alternatives, store catchments and relevant products separately; these are research questions, not published CMA findings.

Documents to examine: CMA case; store-level and customer data; decision when published.

Finance

Question: Can the buyer fund the offer under alternative liquidity scenarios?

Compare the bridge and revolving facilities, available commitments, FX conversion, draw conditions, repayment needs and covenant capacity.

Documents to examine: Amended bridge credit agreement; RCF amendments; cash confirmation.

Regulated business

Question: What change-of-control and consumer protection questions should be tested?

Identify the entities, permissions, regulatory conditions and financial crime controls applicable to the actual business; do not assume approvals.

Documents to examine: Scheme conditions; regulatory permissions; compliance policies.
