Acquisition mechanics

Documented finding

Aviva acquired the entire issued and to-be-issued share capital through a Part 26 scheme. The scheme required a class vote, a separate general meeting resolution and court sanction. Completion occurred on 1 July 2025, distinct from the board agreement in December 2024.

  • Direct Line Part I §2; Part II §§2, 15
  • Aviva Completion announcement

Price bridge and dilution

Documented finding

The fixed exchange ratio was 0.2867 Aviva shares plus 129.7p cash per Direct Line share; permitted dividends were separate. At a 489.3p reference Aviva share price, the offer implied 275p including the maximum 5p dividend, a 73.3% premium to Direct Line’s 27 November 2024 close. The scheme document estimated that target shareholders would hold about 12.5% of the combined company. Those are announcement-basis calculations, not the realised market value on settlement.

  • Direct Line Part I §2
  • Aviva Consideration on effectiveness

Funding and conditions

Documented finding

The scheme describes a bridge facility and the bidder advisers’ resources confirmation. Admission of the new shares and court sanction were conditions. The initial long-stop date was 31 December 2025, with a specified extension mechanism for Phase 2 review. The CMA in fact cleared the deal on 1 July 2025.

  • Direct Line Indicative timetable note 8; Part II §6; Part III
  • CMA Clearance decision

Mandate scope

Documented finding

Slaughter and May advised Direct Line on the recommended acquisition. The separate 2026 PRA matter is a different mandate and must not be counted as a second acquisition advisory role.

  • Slaughter and May Acquisition mandate

Public M&A

Question: How do the scheme conditions, court process and share issuance interlock?

A signed agreement does not transfer ownership; the closing checklist must reflect the legal sequence and settlement obligations.

Documents to examine: Scheme Part III; court order; admission and registrar delivery.

Financing and valuation

Question: What is the value of the consideration if Aviva's share price moves?

A fixed exchange ratio transfers market-price risk to target shareholders. Model cash, shares and dividends separately; do not compare a pre-announcement premium with closing proceeds.

Documents to examine: Scheme Part I §2; bridge agreement summary; offer resources confirmation.

Insurance and prudential risk

Question: What historical reserving, capital and operational risks could survive completion?

Prudential approval is not an indemnity for historical liabilities; validate capital models, claims reserves, reinsurance recoverables, governance and data quality before integration.

Documents to examine: Regulatory approvals; financial statements; separate PRA outcome.

Integration

Question: Which policyholder, systems and employment workstreams need sequencing?

Define controls for access to customer data, complaints, claims continuity and accountable management during migration.

Documents to examine: Integration plan; data-sharing agreements; operational resilience plan.
