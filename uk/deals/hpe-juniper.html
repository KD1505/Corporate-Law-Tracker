Structure

Announcement
2024-01-09
Financing
Signing term loan commitments were expected to be replaced in part by debt, mandatory convertible preferred securities and cash. The April 2025 SEC filing identified $6.5bn senior notes subject to special mandatory redemption if the merger failed to close by the contractual trigger.
Regulatory position
UK clearance and US DOJ settlement are distinct. The DOJ settlement announced June 2025 included divestiture and licensing commitments; exact subsequent implementation not established here.
Financial advisers
J.P. Morgan and Qatalyst for HPE; Goldman Sachs for Juniper.

Commercial implications Analysis

The buyer described networking as a larger and higher-margin part of the combined business. But enterprise wireless competition created a US execution obstacle after UK clearance. The financing redemption trigger connects acquisition timing to debt-market risk: a failed deal could require redemption of bonds even though acquisition consideration was never paid.

What the lawyers may work on Illustrative

Diligence enterprise contracts, software rights, supply chain and customer support obligations. Coordinate deal conditions, cross-border regulatory timelines and integration planning.
