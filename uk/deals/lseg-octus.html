Price and exit

Documented finding

LSEG and Permira announce an agreed $3.535bn cash acquisition. Permira describes full realisation of its funds' investment. Expected H1 2027 completion remains subject to regulatory approval.

Operating profile

Forecast / announced expectation

The announced 2026 target revenue forecast exceeds $300m, with adjusted EBITDA margin around 40%. These are forecasts, not audited actuals. Data subscriptions make retention, licence rights and customer concentration critical diligence questions.

  • LSEG Target financial profile
  • Octus Transaction announcement

Leverage and integration

Forecast / announced expectation

LSEG intends external debt financing. Management forecasts year-end 2027 leverage near the top of 1.5–2.5x target range, subject to Octus and Trumid completion, forecast cash flows and buyback assumptions. Expected adjusted EPS neutrality in year one and accretion thereafter remain projections.

Practical legal workstreams

Original analyst diligence and execution questions. These are not a claim that particular clauses were agreed or that a firm performed these tasks.

Data rights

Question: Does Octus control the data it sells?

Examine source licences, redistribution, derived products, third-party restrictions and employee IP.

Documents to examine: Material licences; IP assignments; provenance register.

Revenue quality

Question: How resilient is the subscription model?

Test retention, renewal, churn, concentration, deferred revenue and change-of-control rights; reconcile forecasts to audited results.

Documents to examine: Customer contracts; revenue cohort data; accounts.

Regulation

Question: Could integration restrict access to critical credit data?

Evaluate overlap, bundling and financial regulatory perimeter without assuming authority findings.

Documents to examine: Regulatory submissions; product map.

Financing

Question: What if growth disappoints?

Stress interest costs, leverage, buybacks and integration costs; coordinate debt availability and closing conditions.

Documents to examine: Financing terms; cash flow model; acquisition agreement.
