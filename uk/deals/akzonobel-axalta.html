Cross-border M&A25 Nov 2026
Chemicals and coatingsPhase 1; undertakings under consideration

On 25 November 2026 the CMA indicated that a proposed disposal of AkzoNobel's vehicle refinish coatings business across the EEA, UK and Switzerland might resolve UK competition concerns. The proposal is not an approval. The parties originally agreed an all-share combination in November 2025.

Transaction perimeter and initial valuation

Documented finding

The November 2025 joint release put the enterprise value of the combined business at around US$25bn. This is a valuation of the combination, not a cash price paid for Axalta.

Initial exchange mechanics

Documented finding

At signing, the parties disclosed 0.6539 AkzoNobel shares for each Axalta share and anticipated 55%/45% ownership for AkzoNobel/Axalta holders. These are original announcement terms, not a verification of subsequent amendments.

  • AkzoNobel and Axalta Transaction Details

UK remedy proposal

Documented finding

The CMA identified concerns in UK vehicle refinish coatings and is assessing a proposed sale of the relevant AkzoNobel business across the EEA, UK and Switzerland. A suitable purchaser and final acceptance remain outstanding.

  • CMA 25 November press release: remedy scope and procedure
