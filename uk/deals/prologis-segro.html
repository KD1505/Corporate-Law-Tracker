Public offer and conditions

Documented finding

The recommended combination provides 0.0920 Prologis shares for each SEGRO share, with a partial cash alternative. The bidder's Rule 2.7 announcement and target's later trading update establish agreement and shareholder approval; the October CMA invitation does not establish regulatory clearance or completion.

  • Prologis Recommended offer terms
  • SEGRO Q3 shareholder approval and expected timing
  • CMA Invitation to comment

Funding mechanics

Documented finding

Prologis's quarterly filing describes a £3.6bn unsecured term loan, conditional on closing and other conditions, drawable in sterling or euros. The maturity is one year after closing, with two possible one-year extensions subject to conditions and fees. At the reported rating the applicable margin was 0.7%, above the relevant SONIA or EURIBOR benchmark. Commitment is not a statement of amount drawn.

Commercial scale

Documented finding

Linklaters described an approximately £14bn offer and a global logistics property platform. SEGRO's Q3 update recorded 33% loan-to-value and £1.2bn available liquidity as separate target metrics. Do not confuse portfolio assets or management scale with the consideration or the acquiring group's leverage.

Public M&A

Question: How does a partial cash alternative affect shareholder elections and allocation?

The cash option may be limited and subject to scaling. Model election and exchange mechanics using the filed Rule 2.7 terms rather than assuming each holder can choose unlimited cash.

Documents to examine: Rule 2.7 announcement; scheme; election forms.

Real estate and REIT tax

Question: Which entity-level tax and asset-level title issues matter in a global logistics portfolio?

REIT status, joint venture governance, lease security, change-of-control and asset valuation affect economics across jurisdictions.

Documents to examine: Property and JV schedules; REIT tax opinions; debt and guarantees.

Finance

Question: What happens if a bridge/term loan maturity falls before permanent financing is available?

Model drawdown CPs, extension fees, interest benchmark mismatch, ratings sensitivity, refinancing and FX risk.

Documents to examine: Form 10-Q term loan disclosure; executed facility agreement.

Competition and closing

Question: Which markets and geographies should be tested before integration?

Logistics property markets can be local and asset-specific. Check overlap and substitute sites, and keep competitively sensitive information controlled pending regulatory clearance.

Documents to examine: CMA case; competition filings; clean-team protocol.
