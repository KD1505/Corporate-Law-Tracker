Commercial implications Analysis

Reinvestment can align the seller and management with the buyer’s growth strategy, but the documents must address governance, leaver provisions, future capital needs and exit mechanics. A majority investment does not imply acquisition of every share.

What the lawyers may work on Illustrative

Negotiate governance and management incentive documents alongside the SPA, review acquisition pipeline and integration risk, identify change-of-control consents in regulated testing and compliance businesses.

Next milestone

Closing, subject to the announced timetable, and any publicly disclosed governance arrangements.
