Acquisition perimeter

Documented finding

The October ministerial statement records agreed terms announced on 6 July for up to £1.6bn. The target is ITV's Media and Entertainment business rather than the entirety of ITV plc. The parties describe a separate five-year £2.1bn ITV Studios content supply arrangement; it is not acquisition consideration.

  • UK Parliament / DCMS Transaction background
  • Sky and ITV Programming commitments

Statutory procedure

Documented finding

The 4 November notice invokes section 42 of the Enterprise Act 2002. Under sections 44 and 44A, CMA and Ofcom must report by 5pm on 27 January 2027, on competition/jurisdiction and media public interest respectively. This is not a clearance or prohibition.

  • DCMS Operative notice and deadline
  • CMA Merger case

Media plurality and programme availability

Documented finding

The notice identifies freedom of expression, plurality of news views and ownership/control as public-interest issues under section 58. It also identifies diversity and quality of broadcasting and on-demand content as a further consideration not already specified in section 58 at issue date.

  • DCMS Enumerated public-interest considerations

Party commitments

Party-stated rationale

Sky and ITV describe preserving free-to-air viewing, separate editorial voices and ITV Studios content supply. These are stated intentions, not remedies accepted by the authorities.
