Practical legal workstreams

Original analyst diligence and execution questions. These are not a claim that particular clauses were agreed or that a firm performed these tasks.

Capital markets

Question: What risks arise from coordinating the acquisition and an accelerated bookbuild?

Disclosure of inside information, underwriting commitments, allocation and admission must align with funding certainty. Confirm the exact UK Listing Rules classification in the issuer's filed announcement.

Documents to examine: Equity issue announcement; underwriting agreement; admission notices.

Debt and FX

Question: How can GBP capital fund a USD purchase price?

Build a sources-and-uses and currency conversion schedule. Review hedging strategy, funding CPs, revolver purpose, debt repayment and refinancing flexibility.

Documents to examine: Facilities, FX instruments and treasury policy (not reviewed).

Technology diligence

Question: Which vendor authorisations and customer contracts drive GDT's economics?

Reseller and distribution contracts may restrict transfer, pricing or territory. Test material relationships, revenue concentration, cybersecurity and data obligations.

Documents to examine: Vendor and customer contracts; software licence register.

Cross-border execution

Question: What approvals and accounting workstreams need US and UK coordination?

Map applicable antitrust and foreign investment triggers, employment and tax structure, and reporting controls; avoid assuming filings solely from deal size.

Documents to examine: Transaction agreement; jurisdictional filing matrix; reporting integration plan.
