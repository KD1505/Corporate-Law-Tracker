Commercial implications Analysis

A professional-services combination can broaden client offerings and geographic reach, but value depends on partner and employee retention, integration and regulatory constraints. Combined turnover is not enterprise or equity value.

What the lawyers may work on Illustrative

Review ownership and regulatory permissions, partner arrangements, client engagement terms, conflicts, confidentiality, tax structuring and integration risk. Assess closing conditions and retention arrangements.
