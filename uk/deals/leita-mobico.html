Commercial implications Analysis

The gap between equity and enterprise valuation makes the capital structure central to the investment thesis. Do not infer net debt from the gap without checking the valuation definitions. A scheme requires its own shareholder and court process; lenders also need certainty on funding and closing.

What the lawyers may work on Illustrative

Model equity bridge and existing debt treatment, test cash confirmation and financing conditions, prepare scheme timetable and coordinate regulatory work. Review change-of-control clauses in transport concessions.
