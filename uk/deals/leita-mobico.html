Commercial implications Analysis

The gap between equity and enterprise valuation makes the capital structure central to the investment thesis. Do not infer net debt from the gap without checking the valuation definitions. A scheme requires its own shareholder and court process; lenders also need certainty on funding and closing.

What the lawyers may work on Illustrative

Model equity bridge and existing debt treatment, test cash confirmation and financing conditions, prepare scheme timetable and coordinate regulatory work. Review change-of-control clauses in transport concessions.

Bidder ownership and target perimeter

Documented finding

Leita Mobility is an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Autobuses de León. Freshfields identifies the joint ownership of that entity by the Cosmen family’s investment interests and an Asterion-managed fund; the family was already Mobico’s largest shareholder. Mobico is a diversified international transport operator, so the buyer-side ownership structure matters to the governance analysis.

Valuation

Question: Build a bridge from £308m equity value to £2.3bn implied enterprise value.

The gap requires reconciliation of net debt, leases, pensions, minorities and any other adjustments according to the offer definitions; a headline subtraction is not a debt diligence conclusion.

Documents to examine: Offer announcement, debt schedule, lease liabilities and defined terms.

Conflicts and governance

Question: How should an existing major shareholder's participation in the acquiring consortium affect the process?

Review independent board decision-making, adviser independence, shareholder voting eligibility and related arrangements. Do not assume the specific conflict procedures without the scheme.

Documents to examine: Scheme document and board recommendation; irrevocable undertakings.

Acquisition finance

Question: How should existing high yield debt, lender CPs and hedging align with closing?

A leveraged buyout may require debt repayment, consent, hedge novation and new funding at the same time; lender counsel's cross-practice team points to relevant workstreams, not confirmed contractual terms.

Documents to examine: Financing documents and public cash confirmation, not yet reviewed.

Transport operations

Question: Which public contracts and licences could have change-of-control constraints?

Review concessions, regulated operating licences, public procurement contracts and vehicle fleet financing across relevant countries.

Documents to examine: Operating contracts; consent register; local law diligence.
