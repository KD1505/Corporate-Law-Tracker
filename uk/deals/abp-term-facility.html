Commitment split

Documented finding

The £300m term facility comprises £200m National Wealth Fund and £100m from a private group led by Bank of America with Lloyds and NatWest. Two-thirds is public commitment; commitments are not drawings.

  • National Wealth Fund 3 June public commitment and eligible projects
  • Linklaters borrower counsel 8 June facility and banks

Eligible projects

Documented finding

The lender identifies Immingham freight terminal, Lowestoft offshore wind, Ipswich Sizewell C supply chain and Solent Gateway among eligible projects.
