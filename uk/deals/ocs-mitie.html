Scheme mechanics and consideration

Documented finding

Mitie is incorporated in Scotland. The scheme document specifies a Scottish court-sanctioned Part 26 process, 218.5p per share payable by OCS plus a separate FY26 dividend of 3.1p. The published £3.1bn acquisition value includes the latter. Linklaters' experience disclosure independently identifies the £3.1bn recommended acquisition, but does not verify the individual components.

  • Mitie Part Two §2 and Part Seven §15.3 (printed p. 106)
  • Linklaters Transaction experience: headline acquisition value

Formal CMA review

Documented finding

The case opened for comments on 15 September; consultation closed on 29 September. Formal Phase 1 commenced on 18 November, with a published decision deadline of 20 January 2027 subject to possible extension. This is an open inquiry, not a clearance.

  • CMA Statutory timetable and Phase 1

Practical legal workstreams

Original analyst diligence and execution questions. These are not a claim that particular clauses were agreed or that a firm performed these tasks.

Public scheme

Question: Which conditions remain despite reported shareholder support?

Build a condition tracker for regulatory clearances, court sanction and closing deliverables. A 99.7% vote does not extinguish competition review.

Documents to examine: Scheme document; interim results; CMA timeline.

Competition

Question: How should a facilities-management overlap be analysed?

Tender data, customer switching, bundling, service categories and contract renewal intervals may inform market definition and competitive effects. These are diligence questions, not CMA findings.

Documents to examine: CMA commencement notice; bid/tender datasets; customer contracts.

Finance

Question: What changed in the financing amendments dated 28 August?

Use the latest documents to reconcile availability periods, CPs, mandatory repayment and long-stop alignment. Do not infer changes merely from the existence of an amendment.

Documents to examine: Original and amended financing documents in issuer hub.

Contracts and workforce

Question: Which customer consents, public procurement and employee obligations could affect integration?

A service business depends on contract continuity and staff deployment. Review change of control, termination, service levels, pensions and TUPE applicability, depending on the integration steps.

Documents to examine: Material contract schedules; employment and pension diligence; integration plan.
