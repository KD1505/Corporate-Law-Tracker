Offer and closing

Documented finding

The agreed cash acquisition of Virgin Money was announced on 21 March 2024 at about £2.9bn. The CMA cleared the transaction in July, and completion followed on 1 October. The later transfer of banking business in April 2026 was not the acquisition closing.

Separate business transfer

Documented finding

Slaughter and May disclosed that the High Court sanctioned the later Part VII transfer of Clydesdale Bank’s business on 23 February 2026, effective 2 April. That statutory transfer followed the acquisition of its parent; share ownership and transfer of the underlying bank business are different legal events.

Practical legal workstreams

Original analyst diligence and execution questions. These are not a claim that particular clauses were agreed or that a firm performed these tasks.

Bank ownership

Question: Why buy the listed parent first and later transfer the bank business?

Corporate control and the transfer of regulated business are different mechanisms, with different stakeholders and approvals. Build a separate dependency map for each.

Documents to examine: Acquisition scheme and subsequent transfer scheme.

Prudential regulation

Question: How do capital, liquidity and governance workstreams change at group and regulated-entity levels?

A group acquisition can leave distinct regulated entities and obligations until later transfer. Counsel should map permissions and supervisory engagement rather than assume they merge automatically.

Documents to examine: Regulatory approvals; transfer court order; entity-level accounts.

Competition

Question: How would overlapping current accounts, mortgages and savings be tested?

Consider switching, segment-specific alternatives and distribution channels. Clearance is not a general finding that every market is unconcentrated.

Documents to examine: CMA case; decision reasons.

Customer migration

Question: Which contractual and operational steps are necessary before customers move?

Business transfer can affect deposits, notices, payment instructions, consumer communications and complaint handling. Coordinate legal and operational readiness.

Documents to examine: Transfer scheme; customer communications; migration and resilience plan.
