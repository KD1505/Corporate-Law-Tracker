Commercial implications Analysis

A growth investment in a founder-led services business may finance expansion without a complete sale. The commercial relationship depends on founder alignment, customer concentration and the rights attaching to the investor's stake.

What the lawyers may work on Illustrative

Review subscription or purchase mechanics, governance and reserved matters, management incentives, IP and key contracts, employment, tax and exit rights. Do not assume a majority stake.
