Commercial implications Analysis

The transferred assets serve one region, but the CMA assessed competition nationally. A remedy must address viable competitive alternatives and patient-service continuity rather than simply transfer nominal assets. The CMA found entry or expansion difficult; this is the regulator's assessment, not a prediction of a final decision.

What the lawyers may work on Illustrative

Assess regional supply and tender alternatives, continuity of patient services and viability of possible remedies; do not assume divestment terms.

Phase 1 and remedy deadline

Documented finding

The case opened 11 August and formal inquiry launched 24 September. The 19 November Phase 1 finding requires acceptable undertakings to avoid Phase 2; the summary states 26 November for offers. This is not a final prohibition.
