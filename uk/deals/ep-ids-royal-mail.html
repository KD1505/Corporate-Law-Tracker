Commercial implications Analysis

The target combines UK postal services and international parcel operations. The government golden share reserves consent rights over specified matters rather than a conventional economic interest. A buyer must distinguish commercially negotiated conditions, statutory national-security protections and contractual undertakings to government. The 370p shareholder value includes 10p dividends; using it as the bidder cash price overstates the cash component.

What the lawyers may work on Illustrative

Map offer acceptance and compulsory acquisition mechanics, separate NSI conditions from negotiated commitments, review regulated postal obligations and workforce arrangements.

Separate government safeguards

Commercial perimeter

Documented finding

The deal involves a holding company with Royal Mail and GLS; commitments protecting UK postal operations should not be treated as identical to controls over the entire international parcel business.

  • EP UK Bidco offer document 26 June 2024; Part 1 §§2 and 13; Part 2
  • UK Government 16 December 2024 binding commitments and golden share

Offer valuation and consideration

Documented finding

The executed deed records 370p total per share, comprising 360p bidder cash and dividends of 2p and 8p. The fully diluted equity value is £3.587bn; implied EV is £5.303bn. Neither is the actual cash required to buy shares not already held by VESA.

  • EP UK Bidco / UK Government deed 16 December 2024; recitals A–E

Acquisition finance and independent financial advice

Documented finding

The formal offer identifies sponsor equity, interim facilities and a cash confirmation from J.P. Morgan. Barclays, BofA and Goldman Sachs advised the target directors under Rule 3.

  • EP UK Bidco offer document 26 June 2024; Part 1 §§2 and 13; Part 2

Government commitments versus Code undertakings

Documented finding

The signed deed expressly distinguishes its commitments to the Secretary of State from Rule 19.5 post-offer undertakings. The NSI final order is a separate statutory instrument.

  • EP UK Bidco / UK Government deed 16 December 2024; recitals A–E
  • UK Government 19 December NSI final order
