Commercial implications Analysis

The target combines UK postal services and international parcel operations. The government golden share reserves consent rights over specified matters rather than a conventional economic interest. A buyer must distinguish commercially negotiated conditions, statutory national-security protections and contractual undertakings to government.

What the lawyers may work on Illustrative

Map offer acceptance and compulsory acquisition mechanics, separate NSI conditions from negotiated commitments, review regulated postal obligations and workforce arrangements.

Separate government safeguards

Documented finding

The government describes golden-share consent rights for headquarters and tax residency. The later NSI final order addresses national security risk relating to Royal Mail's critical services to government.
