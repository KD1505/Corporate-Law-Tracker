Commercial implications Analysis

Buying a development platform may accelerate access to projects and specialist staff but does not guarantee future operating capacity. Value depends on the probability of delivery, grid connections, consenting, capital needs and revenue arrangements.

What the lawyers may work on Illustrative

Diligence project-company ownership, land and option rights, planning status, grid connections, development contracts, workforce and possible contingent consideration; allocate development-stage risks.
