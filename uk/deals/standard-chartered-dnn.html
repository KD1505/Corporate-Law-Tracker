Commercial implications Analysis

This is a debt funding transaction, not a token investment or equity raising. D-FMI uses distributed ledger infrastructure while connecting to established settlement and servicing workflows. The credit exposure remains to the issuer; digital title, settlement finality and operational continuity require separate diligence.

What the lawyers may work on Illustrative

Examine issuance and settlement arrangements, title and payment mechanics, continuity provisions, disclosure and admission requirements. Distinguish application for admission from actual admission.
