Scheme terms and settlement

Documented finding

The parties agreed the recommended cash acquisition on 8 July 2024. On 17 January 2025 the scheme became effective following delivery of the court order. Each eligible share carried 1,290p cash and a 25p special dividend, producing the 1,315p total acquisition value per share. The dividend is not an additional amount on top of 1,315p.

  • Carlsberg Completion

Approval sequence

Documented finding

Regulatory conditions were reported satisfied on 17 December 2024; the court sanctioned the scheme on 15 January 2025 and the scheme became effective two days later. The issuer separately announced expected cancellation of listing and the deadline for cash settlement.

  • Britvic Regulatory conditions and settlement
  • CMA Merger inquiry

Industrial rationale

Party-stated rationale

The acquisition was intended to combine beer and soft-drink operations in the UK. Linklaters reported PepsiCo support and anticipated a major European bottling platform. The underlying commercial case depends on distribution scale, the bottling relationship and integration; do not treat stated intentions as completed synergies.

Public takeover

Question: Which amounts should the consideration table and shareholder communications show?

A dividend-inclusive headline is not the bidder's entire per-share cash payment. Reconcile the record date, settlement and corporate action.

Documents to examine: Scheme document and effective announcement.

Commercial contracts

Question: What if a bottling or distribution agreement has change-of-control provisions?

Strategic customer/supplier rights can alter deal certainty; diligence must identify consent, exclusivity, territory and termination rights without assuming the actual clauses.

Documents to examine: Bottling contracts (not reviewed); public announcements.

Competition

Question: Where are the horizontal and vertical issues in beverages and distribution?

Assess product substitution, grocery channel leverage, input/customer access and routes to market; distinguish a theory from the CMA's actual findings.

Documents to examine: CMA decision; customer and supplier contracts.

Post-close

Question: How should brand integration and employment risks be controlled?

A portfolio combination does not automatically permit contract migration. Review IP licensing, distribution, pensions, employment and data separation.

Documents to examine: Integration plan; IP schedule; third-party consents.
