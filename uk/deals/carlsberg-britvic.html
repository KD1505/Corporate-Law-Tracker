Carlsberg / Britvic

Consumer / beveragesCompleted

Carlsberg confirmed completion on 17 January 2025 after the CMA cleared the acquisition on 17 December 2024. The combined UK business brings beer and soft-drink distribution together.

Commercial implications Analysis

Combining distribution networks may deliver efficiencies, but commercial value also depends on bottling agreements, brand rights, customer concentration and the ability to integrate supply chains. Expected efficiencies are not realised results.

What the lawyers may work on Illustrative

Review material bottling and brand licence arrangements, scheme documents, regulatory submissions, integration planning and change-of-control consents.
