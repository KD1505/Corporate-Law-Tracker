Modernising corporate reporting: consultation

Corporate governanceConsultation; not law

The government opened a consultation on modernising the corporate reporting framework on 7 September. Its proposals include reconsidering the existing distributable-profits and capital-maintenance regime.

Authority: UK government · Instrument: Consultation · Recorded phase: Consultation

Who should check: Companies and advisers assessing proposals for reporting, distributions and capital maintenance.

Published deadline: 30 Nov 2026 · Check current deadline

Commercial / legal implications: If adopted, a solvency-oriented approach could change how boards evaluate distributions and capital transactions. Existing law remains applicable until changes are enacted and in force. Companies should review reporting and governance processes without assuming that proposals are binding.

Watch: Consultation closes 30 November 2026; monitor response, legislation and commencement.
