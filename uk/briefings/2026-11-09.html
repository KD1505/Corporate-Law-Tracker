Data (Use and Access) Act: phased commencement

Data protectionPhased statutory commencement

Most Part 5 privacy and data protection provisions commenced in February 2026. Regulator governance changes took effect in September; a further commencement instrument specifies provisions brought into force on 9 November.

Authority: UK government / legislation · Instrument: Commencement legislation · Recorded phase: Phased commencement

Who should check: Organisations assessing specific data protection provisions and transitional rules.

Commercial / legal implications: A transaction diligence checklist should identify applicable commencement dates and transitional rules, review processing records, contracts and transfer arrangements, and distinguish legislative amendments from regulatory guidance.

Watch: Read the commencement instrument for the specific provision; do not treat the whole Act as effective on a single date.
