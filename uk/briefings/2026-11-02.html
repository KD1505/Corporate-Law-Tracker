Infrastructure / industrial policy2 Nov 2026

£700m Strategic Sites Accelerator

Infrastructure / industrial policyGovernment funding programme announced

Government announced a programme of about £700m in debt, equity and targeted grants to unlock up to ten major industrial and commercial sites. This is programme-level funding, not a completed investment in any named site.

Commercial / legal implications: Reducing planning, infrastructure and financing barriers can make development sites investable. Counsel will need to distinguish public commitments from private investment, examine grant and subsidy conditions, land rights, procurement and the sequencing of financing and development milestones.

Watch: Site selection, grant or funding decisions and evidence of private capital commitments.
