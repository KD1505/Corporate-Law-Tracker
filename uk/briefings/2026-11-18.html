Economy18 Nov 2026

October inflation release: transport pressure

EconomyDated statistical release

The ONS October release identifies transport, especially motor fuels, as the largest upward contributor to the change in annual CPI inflation.

Commercial / legal implications: Fuel-intensive businesses may face margin pressure where contracts lack effective cost pass-through. Counsel can assess price adjustment, hardship and supplier renegotiation mechanisms without assuming inflation excuses performance.

Watch: Next ONS release and evidence of cost pass-through in company results.

Financial regulation18 Nov 2026

FCA review of investment firms prudential regime

Financial regulationCall for evidence; no rule change

The FCA is seeking views on the IFPR, including ICARA, group consolidation, proportionality and wind-down planning. It expects to consult on possible amendments in 2027.

Authority: FCA · Instrument: Call for evidence · Recorded phase: Call for evidence

Who should check: Investment firms and groups assessing prudential obligations.

Published deadline: 20 Jan 2027 · Check current deadline

Commercial / legal implications: Investment firms should distinguish the existing prudential regime from the proposed review. A group restructuring or acquisition can change the risk perimeter and capital and liquidity analysis, so regulatory due diligence needs current group-level data.

Watch: Feedback deadline 20 January 2027; monitor proposed changes. Existing transitional own-funds provisions expire on 31 December 2026, according to the FCA.
