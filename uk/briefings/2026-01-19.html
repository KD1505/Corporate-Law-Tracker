Public offers and admissions to trading regime

Capital marketsIn force from event date

The new public offers and admissions framework and associated changes to UK listing processes took effect on 19 January 2026, according to the FCA. The FCA also describes a notification requirement for admissions under PRM 1.6.4R.

Authority: FCA · Instrument: Rules / policy statement · Recorded phase: In force

Who should check: Issuers and advisers considering offers or admission to trading under the applicable regime.

Commercial / legal implications: For equity issuers and advisers, prospectus analysis must begin with the relevant offer/admission route and exceptions under the current regime, rather than recycling old templates.

Watch: Check the current FCA Handbook and transitional rules before advising on a particular issuance.
