Commercial / legal implications: Revenue support can improve cash flow predictability for qualifying projects, but winning an auction is not equivalent to securing construction funding. Developers need to test bid economics against financing cost, procurement and delivery risk.

Corporate governance19 Nov 2026

Companies House identity verification: enforcement approach

Corporate governancePublished enforcement guidance

Companies House published its approach to non-compliance with mandatory identity verification. The rollout began in November 2025; applicable deadlines depend on the person’s role and circumstances.

Authority: Companies House · Instrument: Enforcement guidance · Recorded phase: Guidance

Who should check: Directors, persons with significant control and teams managing company filings.

Commercial / legal implications: Deal teams should include identity verification and filing readiness in completion and post-completion checklists. A general rollout date is not a substitute for checking the specific statutory deadline and enforcement guidance.

Watch: Current Companies House guidance for the relevant director or person with significant control.
