Takeover Code scope and transition

Public M&AScope guidance; check transition

The Panel’s revised jurisdictional scope has a transition period from 3 February 2025 until 2 February 2027 for certain companies previously covered. A UK adviser alone does not make an offer subject to the Code.

Authority: Takeover Panel · Instrument: Guidance · Recorded phase: Transition

Who should check: Companies and bidders assessing Code applicability and transition criteria.

Commercial / legal implications: Check incorporation, admission history and transition criteria before selecting the public bid timetable. Do not assume every UK-connected acquisition is a Code transaction.

Watch: Check the Panel’s current applicability guidance for the specific target.
