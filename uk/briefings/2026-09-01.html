ONS completed M&A: reading the market correctly

Market statisticsPublished statistical bulletin

The ONS published its April–June 2026 UK M&A bulletin on 1 September. Provisional completed-control transaction values were £25.4bn inward, £2.7bn outward and £4.2bn domestic. The official series covers qualifying changes of ultimate control of £1m or more, not every financing, minority investment or announced offer.

Commercial / legal implications: A database of announced transactions and financing mandates cannot be compared directly with a completed-control acquisition series. Deal value trends can be dominated by a few unusually large transactions; check counts, direction of investment and revision notes separately.

Watch: Next ONS quarterly release scheduled 1 December 2026; check publication calendar.
