Insurance23 Nov 2026

PRA publishes general insurance stress test results

InsurancePublished supervisory exercise

The PRA published results of its 2026 dynamic general insurance stress test on 23 November. The exercise examined how participating insurers respond to severe shocks; it was designed to examine crisis management as well as financial resilience.

Commercial / legal implications: Insurance clients face interconnected exposures across underwriting, reinsurance, liquidity and counterparty risk. Legal teams can use stress scenarios to test recovery governance, reinsurance contract terms and operational escalation, not treat the exercise as a guarantee of solvency.

Watch: PRA follow-up and firm-level risk management responses. No individual insurer capital conclusion is inferred.
