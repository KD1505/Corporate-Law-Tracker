DMCC Act: consumer enforcement powers

Consumer protectionPart 3 commenced

The commencement regulations brought Part 3 of the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 into force on 6 April 2025, subject to the instrument's provisions.

Authority: UK legislation · Instrument: Commencement legislation · Recorded phase: In force

Who should check: Consumer-facing businesses and advisers assessing Part 3 enforcement powers.

Commercial / legal implications: Consumer-facing acquisitions require scrutiny of marketing, subscription and pricing practices and the applicable enforcement regime. Historic revenue may be less sustainable if built on non-compliant practices.

Watch: Check the relevant substantive consumer rules and their separate commencement dates.
