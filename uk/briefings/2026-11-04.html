FCA Primary Market Bulletin 67: proposed technical-note changes

Capital marketsGuidance consultation

GC26/8 proposes updates to technical notes on prospectus exemptions and supplements, and revocation of other guidance following rule changes. The consultation is not itself a final amendment to binding rules.

Authority: FCA · Instrument: Guidance consultation · Recorded phase: Consultation

Who should check: Issuers and advisers applying prospectus and supplementary prospectus rules.

Published deadline: 9 Dec 2026 · Check current deadline

Commercial / legal implications: Issuer counsel should check the operative PRM rules and current guidance when assessing the need for a prospectus or supplement. Updating drafting precedents based solely on proposed notes risks prematurely applying a change.

Watch: Comments due 9 December 2026; monitor finalised guidance.
