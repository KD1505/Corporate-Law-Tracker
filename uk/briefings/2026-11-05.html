Economy5 Nov 2026

Bank Rate rises to 4%

EconomyDated policy decision

At the meeting ending 4 November 2026, the MPC voted 8–1 to raise Bank Rate by 25 basis points to 4%.

Commercial / legal implications: Floating-rate borrowers may face higher debt service. Acquisition models should revisit interest cover, covenant headroom and refinancing assumptions; fixed-rate borrowers may be less immediately affected.

Watch: Next MPC decision, lender pricing and borrower hedging requirements.

Employment5 Nov 2026

Employment Rights Act implementation: 2027 changes

EmploymentImplementation timetable; prospective measures

The published government timetable identifies 1 January 2027 for a reduction in the ordinary unfair dismissal qualifying period to six months and removal of the compensatory award cap, subject to the relevant legal provisions.

Authority: UK government · Instrument: Implementation timetable · Recorded phase: Prospective measures

Who should check: Employers and transaction teams assessing prospective employment changes.

Commercial / legal implications: Employers should update risk assumptions for workforce restructurings, probation and dismissal processes. Deal teams should review employment liabilities and integration costs rather than assume a proposed or prospective rule is already in force.

Watch: Check current commencement and transitional provisions before applying to an individual dismissal.
