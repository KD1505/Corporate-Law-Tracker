What the evidence means

Source-linked
A specific source URL is attached. Its presence does not establish that each underlying claim was independently verified.
Claim-linked
Financial figures, adviser roles and recorded chronology carry individual source links. Read the referenced document and its context.
Editorial review date
The stored review date of a curated record; not a guarantee that all sources were audited or that the status is live.
Independent verification
Separate evidence-backed checking of a particular claim. No corpus-wide independent verification rate is asserted.
Discovery records
Automated titles, metadata and source URLs, not curated transaction facts.

Financial values and omissions

Every disclosed figure has a label, currency where applicable, unit, disclosure qualifier, numerical value where established and attached source. Enterprise value, equity consideration, financing commitments, per-share terms and proposed values are different quantities. Unestablished values are not zero; an explicit statement of no cash is recorded separately.

Sources and retrieval

Use the live source desk to see the latest recorded collection results. Retrieval dates, source update dates and editorial review dates are distinct.

  • CMA cases — Primary regulatory decisions. Public GOV.UK; discovery adapter below. Case evidence separate from transaction completion.
  • FCA NSM — Issuer disclosures. Public search and export; automated reuse/access conditions require review.
  • Takeover Panel — Public offer disclosure. Reference link; not an integrated feed.
  • Bank of England — Monetary policy. Dated editorial source; no live rate feed.
  • ONS — Economic statistics. Dated editorial source; no live series integration.
  • SRA — England and Wales qualification. Official guidance; not automated.
  • GOV.UK Search API — Discovery. See the source monitor for the latest retrieval result and failures.
  • GOV.UK Content API — Regulator source dossiers. Structured source metadata and document links; independent from reviewed editorial data.
  • Issuer and counsel announcements — Editorial primary evidence. Reviewed manually for scope, values and adviser roles; automated collection requires source-specific review.
  • Bank of England news and PRA RSS — Automated source desk. Link and title monitoring, distinct from curated analysis.
  • FCA RSS — Automated source desk. Selected non-warning news links; editorial review remains separate.
  • Government policy sources — Automated source desk. Treasury, Companies House, CMA, and business department notices via GOV.UK Search API.
  • SEC issuer filings — Original issuer filings and transaction exhibits. Use transaction-specific filing or issuer-hosted filing; primary corporate disclosures are not independent assurance.
  • Issuer offer document registers — Scheme documents, conditions and financing disclosures. Document-level review with explicit page or section anchors.
  • Counsel announcements — Mandate discovery. Firm disclosures establish their own client and scope; a second publication of the same statement is not independent corroboration.
