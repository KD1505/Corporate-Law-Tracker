JSA advises Aseem Infrastructure Finance on sanctioning ₹255 crore to Clean Max group hybrid power projects

Banking & Finance 31 Jul 2026· ₹255 cr · Announced· ◔ Reported

Aseem Infrastructure Finance Limited has extended a rupee term loan facility of up to ₹255 crore to Clean Max Teton Private Limited, Clean Max Emerald Private Limited and Clean Max Victoria Private Limited, SPVs of Clean Max Enviro Energy Solutions Limited. JSA Advocates & Solicitors advised Aseem Infrastructure Finance on this financing transaction. The transaction team comprised of Karan Mitroo (Partner), Kartikeya Dubey (Partner), Aditya Bhayal (Senior Associate) and Harsh Dugar (Associate). The funds will be used for the development of solar-wind hybrid power projects in Tamil Nadu and Maharashtra.
