Harshbeena Sahney Zaveri files Regulation 29(2) disclosure of substantial acquisition in NRB Bearing Limited

Regulatory 29 Jun 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

Harshbeena Sahney Zaveri, an individual acquirer, has made a mandatory disclosure under Regulation 29(2) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations 2011 with respect to NRB Bearing Limited, a publicly listed industrial (bearing manufacturing) company. The disclosure was submitted to NSE on 29 June 2026. The specific threshold crossed, the exact percentage acquired, the consideration amount, and the acquisition date are contained within the formal Regulation 29(2) disclosure form (DIS-3 or DIS-4) filed with the exchange and are not disclosed in the headline notice.

Why it mattersRegulation 29(2) disclosures are triggered by crossing 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 25%, 30%, 40%, 50%, or 90% thresholds; the filing itself does not disclose the holding percentage, transaction value or date—those details are contained in the full form filed with the exchange and must be cross-referenced with company announcements and exchange notice boards.
