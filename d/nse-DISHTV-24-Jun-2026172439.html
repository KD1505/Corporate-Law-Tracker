Promoter Group Consortium Files SEBI Takeover Disclosure for Dish TV India

M&A / JV 24 Jun 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

The disclosure filed with NSE on 23–24 Jun 2026 involves a multi-party consortium comprising corporate vehicles (Direct Media Distribution Ventures, Veena Investments, World Crest Advisors, JSGG Infra Developers), a Mauritius-resident holding company (Agrani Holdings), and individual promoters (Gaurav Goel, Priti Poddar, Jai Goel, Jawahar Lal Goel, Nishi Goel, Suryansh Goel, Sushila Devi). The filing is lodged under Regulation 31(4) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011, which requires disclosure of concert-party acquisitions or acquisition proposals. No consideration, target shareholding or timeline for completion is disclosed in the NSE filing;…

Why it mattersReg 31(4) disclosure is mandatory when persons acting in concert effect or propose a substantial acquisition; this filing flags potential concert-party acquisition mechanics and triggers formal review of shareholding patterns, disclosure timelines and open-offer conditionality under SEBI Takeover Code.
