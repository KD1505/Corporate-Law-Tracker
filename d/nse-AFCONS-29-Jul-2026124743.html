Goswami Infratech files substantial acquisition disclosure for Afcons Infrastructure under SEBI takeover code

M&A / JV 29 Jul 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

Goswami Infratech Private Limited submitted a Regulation 31(1) disclosure to NSE, indicating a substantial acquisition of Afcons Infrastructure Limited shares. Regulation 31(1) of the SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011 requires disclosure once an acquirer crosses prescribed thresholds (typically 5%, and further at 10%, 15%, etc.). The disclosure does not specify the exact shareholding percentage, acquisition value, consideration structure, or timeline. This is a preliminary public filing; further announcements and ongoing acquirer disclosures are expected.

Why it mattersRegulation 31(1) disclosure signals a material (typically 5%+ or higher threshold) crossing that will trigger open market acquisition obligations and ongoing reporting requirements; the acquirer's identity and timing suggest either a strategic infrastructure consolidation or a private-equity-backed acquisition in the construction/infrastructure space.
