Sammaan Capital Limited: Timely Interest Payment Certification on Listed Debentures

Regulatory 23 Jun 2026 · In force· ✓ Verified

Sammaan Capital Limited, a non-banking financial company, issued both Secured and Unsecured Redeemable Non-Convertible Debentures via public issue and listed them on stock exchanges. On 23 June 2026, the company certified to the NSE that it had made timely payment of all accrued interest on these debentures in full compliance with SEBI Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements Regulations, 2015. This is a routine mandatory disclosure under Regulation 57, triggered after each interest payment date, to certify absence of default or payment delay.

Why it mattersRegulation 57 certification is a routine disclosure gating item for NBFC debt issuers post-interest payment; routine compliance filing indicates no covenant breach or payment default events requiring material disclosures.
