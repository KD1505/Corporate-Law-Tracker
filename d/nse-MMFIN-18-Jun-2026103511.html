Mahindra & Mahindra Limited files SEBI Takeover disclosure for MMFSL stake

M&A / JV 15 Jun 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

The disclosure was submitted to NSE as a regulatory filing under SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011. Regulation 31(4) mandates disclosure of substantial acquisitions of shares, typically when thresholds of 25%, 35%, 50%, or 75% are breached. The specific consideration amount, acquisition date, and exact shareholding percentage acquired remain to be disclosed in the full regulatory filing. This filing suggests a structured acquisition event within the MMFSL capital structure.

Why it mattersSEBI Regulation 31(4) triggers disclosure obligation when acquirer crosses 25%/35%/50%/75% thresholds; this filing indicates a material threshold crossed requiring public disclosure and potential open offer implications under the 2011 Takeover Code.
