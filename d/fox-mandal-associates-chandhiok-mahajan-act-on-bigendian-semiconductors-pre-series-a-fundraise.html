Fox Mandal & Associates, Chandhiok & Mahajan act on BigEndian Semiconductors pre-Series A fundraise

PE / VC 15 Jul 2026· $6 mn · Completed· ◔ Reported

Fox Mandal & Associates advised BigEndian Semiconductors on this fundraise. The Firm rendered comprehensive legal support throughout the transaction, including advising on the transaction structure; drafting, reviewing, and negotiating the definitive transaction documents; facilitating their execution; and assisting with the satisfaction of closing conditions and other ancillary matters to ensure the successful completion of the transaction.
