Manavta Holdings files substantial acquisition disclosure for Chambal Fertilizers under SEBI takeover code

M&A / JV 16 Jun 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

Manavta Holdings Limited has submitted a formal disclosure to the NSE under Regulation 31(4) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011. This regulation requires disclosure when a person or group acquires shares in a target company in prescribed tranches or circumstances. The filing was received by the exchange on 16 June 2026. The specific shareholding acquired, consideration, and whether an open offer will follow have not been disclosed in this preliminary disclosure document.

Why it mattersThe filing of a Regulation 31(4) disclosure (rather than a Regulation 31(1) initial open offer trigger) suggests the acquirer has crossed a threshhold triggering continuous disclosure obligations; counsel should verify whether an open offer will follow or if the acquisition remains below the 25% trigger under current shareholding patterns.
