Al Maha and Indigo Infracon acquire 54.84% of Lake Shore Realty; mandatory open offer receives 17 shares

M&A / JV 10 Nov 2025· ₹11.04 cr SPA; ₹5.23 cr offer ceiling · Completed· Document-reviewed case study

Transaction and status (2025): Under a share purchase agreement dated 30 May 2025, Category I foreign portfolio investor Al Maha Investment Fund PCC – ONYX Strategy (Acquirer 1) and Indigo Infracon Private Limited (Acquirer 2) acquired, in aggregate, 19,19,630 fully paid-up ₹10 shares representing 54.84% of the voting share capital of BSE-listed Lake Shore Realty Limited (formerly Mahaan Foods Limited; BSE 519612; CIN L68100DL1987PLC350285), from six selling promoter shareholders. The contractual price was ₹57.51 per share, totalling ₹11,03,97,921.30, payable in cash. The 70:30 distribution between the buyers was Al Maha 13,43,741 shares (38.39% of target voting capital) and Indigo…

Why it mattersListed-company takeover precedent: distinguish the 54.84% jointly acquired under the SPA from Indigo Infracon's individual 16.45% allocation; distinguish a 26% mandatory open-offer CAP from only 17 shares ACTUALLY accepted. Check the 70:30 inter-acquirer MOU, SAST Regulations 3(1), 4, 7, 8, 17, 18(12) and 22(2), full-cash escrow, seller reclassification, control changes, minimum public-shareholding sensitivity and 10-year dairy/branding restrictions. The disclosed 2025 closing does not establish November 2026 ownership after subsequent disposals.

Disclosed SPA covenants and completion mechanics

  • SPA Article 2.1.1: subject to conditions precedent, transfer of sale shares free and clear of encumbrances at ₹57.51 per share
  • SPA Article 3.1.5: pre-closing deposit of 100% of maximum open-offer consideration in cash escrow
  • SPA Article 5.1: transfer of sale shares on-exchange or off-market pursuant to applicable law
  • Completion mechanics: sellers' irrevocable demat delivery instructions and cash paid to designated bank accounts; acquirer nominee directors and control transfer
  • 10 years after completion: target not to undertake dairy production, processing, distribution, marketing or dairy-nutrition business
  • Restrictions on use/registration of Mahaan/MFL names, marks, branding, domain names, goodwill and associated IP
  • SPA Article 7.5: change target registered office within 30 days of completion; LOF records an August 4, 2025 Delhi office change

Verification and interpretation notes

  • The 54.84% was acquired JOINTLY; Indigo Infracon's separate initial SPA allocation was 16.45%, Al Maha's 38.39%.
  • The 26% open offer was a MAXIMUM size, not the actual uptake. 17 shares were accepted.
  • The LOF's 80.83% post-offer holding is ONLY assuming full acceptance; actual 2025 post-offer aggregate was 54.84%.
  • Manager to Offer Almondz Financial Services and Registrar Alankit are NOT identified as legal counsel.
  • Historical 2025 holdings are not current as of November 2026; subsequent shareholder changes must be reconciled independently.
  • Actual acceptance count 17 comes from the original post-offer newspaper advertisement; the SEBI LOF predates tendering and cannot establish offer uptake.
