Markolines Pavement Technologies Limited receives NSE/BSE observation letter for amalgamation/merger

M&A / JV 18 Aug 2026 · In regulatory review· ✓ Verified

Markolines Pavement Technologies Limited, an infrastructure and pavement technology company, announced clearance from both NSE and BSE on its proposed amalgamation/merger on 18 August 2026. The observation letters confirm no regulatory objection from the exchanges, a mandatory step under Regulation 37 of the Listing Rules before a listed company can proceed with a merger scheme. The status of the merger parties (whether one is acquiring the other, or if it is a consolidation) and the consideration have not been disclosed in the exchange filing.
