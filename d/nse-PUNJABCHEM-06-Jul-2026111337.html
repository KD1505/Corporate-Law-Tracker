Shalil S Shroff files substantial acquisition disclosure under SEBI Takeover Code for Punjab Chemicals & Crop Protection

M&A / JV 6 Jul 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

Shalil S Shroff, acting as acquirer, has submitted a statutory disclosure to NSE under Regulation 31(4) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011. This disclosure is mandated whenever a person crosses material shareholding thresholds (5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 25%, etc.) in a listed company. The filing does not yet confirm the exact shareholding percentage, quantum of shares acquired, or consideration, but signals an active acquisition campaign. Depending on the final stake, an open offer (mandatory at 25%) or voluntary offer may follow. No legal advisers are named in the NSE disclosure document itself.

Why it mattersA Reg 31(4) disclosure confirms crossing of the 5% threshold in listed equity; counsel must now track whether an open offer is triggered (typically at ≥25%), confirm exempt categories (if any), and advise on timetables under the Code.
