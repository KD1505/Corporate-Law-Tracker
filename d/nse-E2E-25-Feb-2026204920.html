E2E Networks Limited launches Qualified Institutions Placement (QIP) with floor price of ₹2,630.60 per share

IPO / ECM 25 Feb 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

E2E Networks, a technology infrastructure company, initiated a Qualified Institutions Placement on 25 February 2026 via Board-level Fund Raise Committee approval. The floor price of ₹2,630.60 per equity share was determined in accordance with the SEBI ICDR Regulations 2018 pricing formula (typically a 90-day volume-weighted average price adjustment mechanism). The Preliminary Placement Document (PPD) dated 26 February 2026 was adopted the same day, incorporating the application form and terms of issue.
