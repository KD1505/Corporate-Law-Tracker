Ramco Cements files Scheme of Amalgamation with NSE

M&A / JV 20 Jun 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

Ramco Cements Limited, a major Indian cement manufacturer with multi-state operations, disclosed a Scheme of Amalgamation to NSE on 20 June 2026 indicating structural reorganisation. The scheme document, filed with the exchange as per Listing Rules disclosure norms, sets out merging entities, consideration, and effective date; however, the target entity name and deal valuation have not been disclosed in the announcement stub. Standard regulatory pathway requires NCLT approval under Sections 230–232 of the Companies Act 2013 and shareholder sanction under Section 110; timeline and identities of the amalgamating company and the transferee company remain pending corroboration from NCLT…

Why it mattersA listed cement consolidation of this nature will require dual-track approval—NCLT sanction (Sections 230–232) and shareholder resolution (Section 110)—and may trigger stock exchange listing rules for material related-party transactions and disclosure timelines; monitor NCLT Regional Bench filings and follow-up scheme amendments for consideration structure and merger accounting treatment.
