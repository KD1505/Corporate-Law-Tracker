Ugro Capital: SEBI Takeover Disclosure Filed Under Regulation 31(4)

M&A / JV 23 Jun 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

Poshika Advisory Services LLP submitted to NSE a formal disclosure under Regulation 31(4) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011, concerning Ugro Capital Limited. The filing date of 23 Jun 2026 indicates a substantial acquisition event. Regulation 31(4) requires disclosure when an acquirer reaches or crosses 25% of voting capital, and the disclosure must include acquirer identity, shareholding percentage, consideration, timing and source of funds.

Why it mattersRegulation 31(4) disclosures are required when an acquirer crosses 25% shareholding threshold; this filing establishes compliance with mandatory disclosure obligations and triggers consequential put/call option provisions.
