Suraj Estate Developers Limited enables ₹500 cr fundraise via equity or convertible securities

Regulatory 14 Aug 2026· ₹500 cr · Announced· ✓ Verified

Suraj Estate Developers Limited, listed on NSE, has informed the Exchange of an enabling resolution passed by shareholders authorizing the Board to raise capital up to ₹500 crores through multiple permissible instruments (equity shares, equity-linked securities, convertible securities, warrants) and methods (private placement, preferential issue, rights issue, QIP or other modes permitted by law). The fundraise is subject to shareholder approval (obtained) and necessary regulatory and statutory approvals.
