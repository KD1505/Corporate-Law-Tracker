Dotex Merchandise files substantial acquisition disclosure in PCBL Chemical

M&A / JV 22 Jun 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

Dotex Merchandise Private Limited has submitted a Regulation 31(4) disclosure to NSE under the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011. This disclosure is a mandatory filing when a person acquires shares above specified thresholds (typically 25% or more, or in certain concert-party scenarios). The filing does not yet confirm deal completion, valuation, or exemption status; it marks the formal regulatory notification of acquisition intent or completion. PCBL Chemical Limited is a chemicals and industrial products manufacturer.
