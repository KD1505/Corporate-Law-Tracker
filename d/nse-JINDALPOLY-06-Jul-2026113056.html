Concatenate Flexi Films Advest files substantial acquisition disclosure under SEBI Takeover Code

M&A / JV 6 Jul 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

Concatenate Flexi Films Advest Private Limited submitted a disclosure under Regulation 31(4) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 to the NSE. This is the initial announcement stage of a substantial acquisition transaction. The full details of consideration, target shareholding, conditions and timeline are not available in this excerpt and would be detailed in supplementary regulatory disclosures and stock exchange announcements.

Why it mattersSubstantial acquisition threshold trigger requires comprehensive disclosure under the 2011 Takeover Regulations; practitioners must verify whether the acquisition breaches 25% or 5% thresholds and review additional filings (detailed disclosure under Reg. 31(2) or open offer obligations under Reg. 37).
