Godawari Power and Ispat Limited: SEBI Takeover Disclosure under Regulation 10(6)

M&A / JV 19 Aug 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

N P Agrawal submitted a Regulation 10(6) disclosure report to NSE, indicating an acquisition in GPIL made under an exemption provided under SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011. The filing is procedural-demonstrating that the acquirer met an exemption criterion (e.g., acquisitions below the 25% threshold, or by certain classes of investors) and is now complying with post-acquisition disclosure obligations. Godawari Power and Ispat is an integrated power and steel manufacturer headquartered in Nagpur, Maharashtra, with operations in thermal power and specialty steel production.

Why it mattersRegulation 10(6) exemptions (typically for acquisitions below thresholds, or by persons acting in concert meeting carve-outs) allow substantial stake accumulation without triggering open-offer obligations; this filing documents compliance with disclosure-reporting duty post-acquisition, a key procedural gate in takeover regulation.
