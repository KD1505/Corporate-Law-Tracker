Deepak Builders & Engineers India Limited files SEBI Regulation 31(4) substantial acquisition disclosure with NSE.

M&A / JV 19 Jun 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

Deepak Builders & Engineers India Limited, listed on NSE, filed a Regulation 31(4) disclosure with the exchange on 19 June 2026, indicating a substantial acquisition of shares or change in control. Regulation 31(4) of the SEBI Takeover Regulations mandates disclosure of acquisition events crossing specified thresholds (typically 25% and 75% of voting capital). The nature and parties to the acquisition are contained in the filed disclosure document.

Why it mattersRegulation 31(4) filings are mandatory post-acquisition transparency documents; the filing confirms a shareholding event has occurred, triggering acquirer identification and materiality thresholds under takeover law-partners should obtain the full disclosure PDF to assess whether an open offer obligation (Regulation 26) has been triggered.
