CAC Holdings Corporation files substantial acquisition disclosure for Inspirisys Solutions under SEBI Takeover Regulations

M&A / JV 19 Jun 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

CAC Holdings Corporation, a foreign entity, has submitted a statutory disclosure under Regulations 31(4) and 31(5) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 to NSE, evidencing a crossing of the 5% (or higher slab) disclosure threshold in Inspirisys Solutions Limited. The disclosure filing is a mandatory stepping-stone in India's takeover code framework: it freezes the acquirer's ability to acquire further shares until the public announcement is made and the open-offer window (if triggered) is navigated. The specific shareholding percentage, acquisition date and consideration remain to be confirmed from the full regulatory filing or company press release.

Why it mattersA non-resident acquirer's entry into a listed Indian technology company triggers detailed continuous disclosure obligations and potential open offer thresholds under the 2011 Takeover Regulations - partner must immediately audit shareholding ladder, concert party status and open offer exemptions.
