VERTICES PARTNERS Assists Ledgers Fintech's UAE Expansion

M&A / JV 19 Aug 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

Ledgers Fintech Limited, a subsidiary of EQL Financial Technologies Private Limited (backed by ICICI Bank, BeeNext & Udtara), has expanded into the UAE. VERTICES PARTNERS advised on the expansion, led by Partner Vishal Mehta with Associates Darshan Hampannavar and Darshan Chheda, with strategic input from Founder & Managing Partner Vinayak Burman. Ledgers provides AI-powered accounting, CRM, and financial software services, with platform presence covering invoicing, payroll, VAT, corporate tax filing, and compliance for UAE businesses. EQL had initially launched LEDGERS.Cloud and Filings.AE in the UAE in December 2023.

Why it mattersCross-border fintech platform expansions into UAE require careful structuring under DFSA, DIFC, and Central Bank of UAE frameworks; VERTICES' advisory likely covered regulatory licensing, entity formation, and operational compliance for a B2B SaaS product targeting Gulf entrepreneurs.
