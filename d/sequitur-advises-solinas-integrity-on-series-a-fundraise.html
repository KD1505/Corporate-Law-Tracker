<cite index="1-3">Solinas Integrity Private Limited has raised $5.5 million from a Series A1 fundraise led by Hero Enterprise Partner Ventures and co-led by Mela Ventures</cite>, with <cite index="44-1">Sequitur Advocates & Legal Consultants acting as exclusive legal advisor</cite>.

PE / VC 6 Aug 2026· $5.5 million · Completed· ✓ Verified

The round follows Solinas's Series A, led by SBI Ventures through the SVL-SME Fund (Neev II) in May 2024. Solinas builds robotics and AI systems that inspect, clean, and digitize underground water and wastewater infrastructure. Its technology is currently deployed across more than 35 cities and 15 states in India; the company has also expanded to the Middle East. Capital will be used to expand manufacturing capacity, product development, strengthen software infrastructure, and support working capital requirements as Solinas scales government and enterprise contracts, with government-led infrastructure programmes such as AMRUT, Jal Jeevan Mission, and the NAMASTE scheme driving adoption.

Why it mattersSeries A1 follow-on rounds in climate-tech deeptech—especially capital-intensive hardware-AI hybrids—benefit from investor syndication (conglomerate + VC + family office) and require crisp structuring of IP ownership, founder dilution mechanics, and government subsidy offsets (AMRUT, Jal Jeevan Mission anchoring customer demand).
