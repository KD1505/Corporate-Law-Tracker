Unilever PLC files SEBI Takeover Disclosure for Hindustan Unilever Limited

Disclosure archive · not counted as a separate transaction. Routine compliance/disclosure, not proof of a new economic transaction

M&A / JV 18 Jun 2026 · Filed / open· Official filing linked · facts not fully audited

Unilever PLC, the UK-listed FMCG multinational, filed a formal disclosure with NSE under Regulation 31(4) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011. This regulation requires acquirers to disclose shareholding and intent within two days of crossing specified thresholds (5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 25%, 30%, 40%, 50%, 60%, or 75% of voting capital). The filing indicates a material transaction in Hindustan Unilever, a flagship FMCG subsidiary. Corroborating details on stake percentage, offer price, and regulatory approvals remain to be confirmed from official exchange announcements and the detailed takeover disclosure document.

Why it mattersA Regulation 31(4) disclosure signals a potential delisting or consolidation under SEBI takeover rules; counsel must track the follow-up open offer disclosure (if mandatory) and timeline to understand whether this is a compulsory delisting or partial stake raise.
