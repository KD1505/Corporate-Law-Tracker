Medi Assist Healthcare acquires additional stake in UK subsidiary Mayfair We Care

M&A / JV 24 Jun 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

Medi Assist Healthcare Services Limited, a listed entity, has increased its shareholding in Mayfair We Care Limited, a UK-registered healthcare services company and existing subsidiary. The NSE intimation filed on 24 June 2026 confirms the acquisition but does not disclose the quantum of additional shares, purchase price, or deal structure. This is a cross-border intra-group transaction likely involving regulatory filing with the UK Companies House and potential FEMA approval or exemption review under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme.

Why it mattersSubsidiary stake consolidation by an India-listed healthcare services firm may trigger transfer pricing documentation, remittance policy review under Schedule 4 of the ITA, and potential disclosure obligations under Ind-AS 27 (consolidated statements); confirm whether this is a full acquisition or minority stake top-up.
