Osia Hyper Retail Limited: 3rd CoC Meeting held in CIRP

IBC 25 Jun 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

Osia Hyper Retail Limited, a retail entity, is undergoing CIRP. The third meeting of its Committee of Creditors was originally scheduled for 22 June 2026 at 16:30 IST but was rescheduled to 25 June 2026 at 14:30 IST and conducted in hybrid mode (video conference and physical attendance). The NSE filing confirms the meeting occurrence and reschedule, as mandated under IBC disclosure rules for listed entities.
