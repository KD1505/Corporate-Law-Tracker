JSW Holdings Limited files Regulation 31(4) disclosure under SEBI Takeover Code

Regulatory 16 Jun 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

JSW Holdings Limited, a steel and infrastructure conglomerate listed on NSE, has filed a formal disclosure with the National Stock Exchange under Regulation 31(4) of the SEBI Takeover Code. This regulation requires disclosure when a person or group acquires, whether directly or indirectly, 5% or more of the voting capital of a listed company. The filing has been submitted to NSE and is available in the exchange archives, indicating the company or an acquirer has crossed a material shareholding threshold. The specific details of the acquiring party, the number of shares acquired, and the price/consideration are contained in the full disclosure document (Form A or amended disclosure) filed…

Why it mattersRegulation 31(4) disclosures are mandatory filings triggered by acquisition of 5% or more shares in a listed entity and set the procedural gate for open offers or exemptions; this filing establishes the baseline for any ensuing takeover mechanics or creeping acquisitions and must be cross-checked against insider trading and concert-party rules.
