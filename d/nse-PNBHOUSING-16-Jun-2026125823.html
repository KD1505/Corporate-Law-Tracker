PNB files SEBI Takeover Disclosure for Substantial Acquisition in PNB Housing Finance

M&A / JV 16 Jun 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

PNB submitted formal disclosure to NSE under SEBI (SAST) Regulations 2011 Regulation 31(4), which mandates disclosure within 2 working days of crossing prescribed shareholding thresholds. The disclosure requirement indicates PNB acquired or announced intention to acquire shares in PNB Housing Finance, a listed housing finance entity. Depending on the quantum acquired, this may trigger mandatory open offer at the specified acquisition price, with implications for minority shareholders and potential delisting proceedings.

Why it mattersSubstantial acquisition disclosure under Reg 31(4) activates open offer obligations if acquisition crosses 25% threshold; structural importance lies in whether PNB is acquiring additional stake beyond existing promoter shareholding or consolidating subsidiary holdings post-restructuring.
