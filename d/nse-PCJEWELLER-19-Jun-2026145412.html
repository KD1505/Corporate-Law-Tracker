Shivani Gupta files SEBI Takeover Regulation 31(4) disclosure in PC Jeweller Limited

Regulatory 19 Jun 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

Shivani Gupta, who was subject to a 2021 SEBI insider trading penalty order (Rs 20 lakh fine and two-year trading ban on PC Jeweller securities), has filed the Regulation 31(4) disclosure under the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations 2011 with NSE. Regulation 31(4) requires disclosure of encumbered shareholdings by promoters and promoter groups on an annual or event-driven basis. The exact event triggering this disclosure—whether a threshold crossing, pledge release, inter-se transfer, or annual affirmation—and the shareholding percentage or value is not stated in the available headline.

Why it mattersRegulation 31(4) disclosures track threshold events (5%, 10%, 25%, etc.) in listed company shareholdings; this filing may signal a structural event such as a promoter regrouping, share pledge release, or transfer between promoter family members—common mechanics in family-controlled listed entities to reorganise group holdings or settle historical SEBI enforcement actions.
