JSA assists NaBFID on sanctioning ₹1,500 crore to Citius TransNet

IPO / ECM 10 Jun 2026· ₹1,500 cr · Completed· ◔ Reported

National Bank for Financing Infrastructure and Development (NaBFID) has sanctioned rupee term loan facilities aggregating up to ₹1,500 crore to Citius TransNet Investment Trust. JSA Advocates & Solicitors advised NaBFID on this financing transaction. The transaction was led by Karan Mitroo (Partner), with support from Pragya Verma (Senior Associate) and Jhalak Gupta (Associate).
