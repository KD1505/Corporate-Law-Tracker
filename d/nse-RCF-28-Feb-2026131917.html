RCF fined ₹5.43 lakh by NSE and BSE each for SEBI LODR non-compliance in Q3 FY25-26

Regulatory 27 Feb 2026· ₹5,42,800 per exchange · In force· ✓ Verified

RCF, a public sector undertaking in chemicals and fertilizers, received separate notices of fine from both the National Stock Exchange and BSE for the same quarter-end compliance lapse. The non-compliance relates to Regulation 17(1) of SEBI's Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements (LODR) Regulations, which prescribes board composition and independence requirements. The fine structure—equal amounts imposed by both exchanges—reflects coordinated regulatory action. RCF intimated the exchanges formally of the penalty on or around 28 February 2026.

Why it mattersRegulation 17(1) LODR (board composition and independence norms) non-compliance triggers dual-exchange enforcement action; listed entities must audit Q-end board structure disclosures against current SEBI LODR standards to avoid similar fines.
