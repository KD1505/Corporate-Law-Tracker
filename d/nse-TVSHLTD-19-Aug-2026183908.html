TVS Holdings Limited: Scheme of Arrangement Approved

M&A / JV 19 Aug 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

TVS Holdings Limited, the investment and holding arm of the TVS group conglomerate, has announced NCLT approval of a formal Scheme of Arrangement between the company and its shareholder body. The scheme restructures the capital or operational relationship between the holding company and its investor base. As a holding-company scheme, this typically involves reorganisation of share capital, inter-company transfers, dividend reallocation, or consolidation of investor rights. The announcement confirms NCLT pronouncement (the primary regulatory gate for all schemes under s. 230–232), signalling the scheme has passed both shareholder and creditor sanction phases and awaits final implementation…

Why it mattersScheme of Arrangement approval under the Companies Act 2013 (s. 230–232) by NCLT establishes binding corporate restructuring and capital reorganisation frameworks; practitioners should track implementation mechanics, shareholder payout schedules, and any deferred class benefits embedded in the scheme.
