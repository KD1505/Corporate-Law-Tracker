Sigma Advanced Systems deepens Rolls-Royce partnership with £125m long-term agreement post-Bromford acquisition

M&A / JV 25 Aug 2026· £125 million · Announced· Official filing linked · facts not fully audited

Sigma Advanced Systems, a defence and aerospace component manufacturer, announced the expanded £125 million long-term commercial agreement with UK-listed Rolls-Royce plc (maker of aero-engines and defence systems). The announcement followed the company's strategic acquisition of Bromford Group, signalling that the target's operational footprint or capabilities enhanced Sigma's bid to deepen OEM relationships.
