Vakil family entities file substantial shareholding disclosure under SEBI takeover regulations

Regulatory 30 Jun 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

Nehal Abhay Vakil, Bhairavi Abhay Vakil, and Vivek Abhay Vakil have submitted a disclosure to NSE under Regulation 31(4) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011, signalling a material change in their aggregate shareholding in Asian Paints Limited. The filing is a mandatory disclosure when a person (or group of persons acting in concert) acquires or transfers shares such that their aggregate shareholding crosses the 5% threshold, or when existing substantial shareholders' stakes change materially. The Vakil family are longstanding promoters of Asian Paints; this disclosure may reflect a restructuring, transfer, or consolidation within the promoter…

Why it mattersA Regulation 31(4) disclosure signals a material change in control or shareholding structure; partners advising the Vakil group or Asian Paints must review whether a formal open offer, creeping acquisition exemption, or exemption from substantial acquisition norms applies, and assess disclosure timing and adequacy under Regulation 30 of the Listing Rules.
