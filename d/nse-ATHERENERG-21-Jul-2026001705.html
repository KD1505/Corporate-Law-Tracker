Ather Energy allots 10.81 million shares via QIP

IPO / ECM 21 Jul 2026 · Completed· ✓ Verified

The NSE allotment notice dated 21 July 2026 confirms allotment of 10,815,307 securities to qualified institutional buyers under the QIP framework. Ather Energy, which listed in 2024, conducted this QIP to fund growth initiatives in its electric two-wheeler and battery manufacturing business. The specific issue price and total capital raised are not disclosed in the allotment notice itself; those are typically disclosed in concurrent investor updates or regulatory filings.

Why it mattersQIP structure allows listed companies to raise capital from institutional investors without pre-emptive rights; allotment completion triggers lock-in period obligations (1 year from allotment for promoter/related-party shares if applicable) and enhanced disclosure under SEBI listing rules.
