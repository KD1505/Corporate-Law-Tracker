Legacy Law Offices acts on Atharva Polyplast IPO

IPO / ECM 29 Jul 2026· ₹27 cr · Ruling· ◔ Reported

Atharva Polyplast Limited has successfully completed its SME IPO on the Bombay Stock Exchange SME for a book-built offer for a fresh issue of 45,00,000 shares, with an offer size of precisely ₹27 crore. Legacy Law Offices advised and acted as the legal counsel for Atharva Polyplast and the Book Running Lead Manager Horizon Management Pvt Ltd on this IPO. The transaction was co-led and supervised by Shalini Munjal (Co-Managing Partner and Chair – General Corporate) and Roopali Chaturvedi (Senior Partner – Capital Markets), with support from Naman Anand (Counsel).
