Time Securities Services submits SEBI Takeover Regulation disclosure to NSE

Regulatory 23 Jun 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

Time Securities Services Private Limited, likely a subsidiary or associate of Time Technoplast Limited, has submitted a statutory disclosure to NSE under Regulation 10(6) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011. This regulation requires disclosure of acquisitions made in reliance upon exemptions provided under Regulation 10, such as acquisitions in a rights issue, preferential allotment, or from a pledge enforcement. The filing is a compliance milestone rather than a substantive event announcement, but signals a material transaction structure that avoided the mandatory open-offer threshold (typically 25% under Regulation 4).

Why it mattersReg 10(6) filings signal use of exemptions from open-offer triggers-typically for inter-group reorganisations, preferential issues, or pledged collateral exercises-and practitioners must verify the exemption basis and any associated disclosure obligations to minority shareholders under the SAST framework.
