KPI Green Energy and Raz Holding Group sign Non-Binding Letter of Intent for Strategic Investment and/or Collaboration

M&A / JV 24 Aug 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

KPI Green Energy Limited, a listed renewable energy company, and Raz Holding Group have executed a non-binding Letter of Intent dated 24 August 2026 to explore strategic investment and/or collaboration opportunities. No consideration amount, equity stake, timeline or definitive terms have been disclosed. The LOI is preliminary and does not constitute a binding commitment; a definitive agreement would be subject to due diligence, board approvals, shareholder consent (if material) and regulatory clearances.

Why it mattersNon-binding LOI signals potential institutional/foreign capital interest in renewable energy infrastructure; partners should monitor for binding definitive agreement triggers, regulatory approvals (FDI/FIPB if foreign capital) and disclosure requirements under Listing Rules.
