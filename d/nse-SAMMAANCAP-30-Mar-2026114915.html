Sammaan Capital Limited allots ₹750 crore secured, rated NCDs on private placement basis

Banking & Finance 30 Mar 2026· ₹750 cr · Completed· ✓ Verified

Sammaan Capital Limited (formerly IHFL) completed allotment of 75,000 rated, secured, redeemable NCDs at ₹1,00,000 face value on 30 March 2026, totalling ₹750 crore. The debentures are listed and were issued via private placement, approved by the Securities Issuance and Investment Committee on 24 March 2026. The structure as secured and rated NCDs aligns with regulatory expectations for housing finance/retail lending entities operating under tightened NBFC guidelines post-IL&FS crisis.

Why it mattersThe private placement of secured, listed NCDs signals continued non-bank fintech debt capital markets access post-rebranding and under tightened lending regulations; the secured rating structure (vs. unsecured) reflects lender risk repricing in the retail lending space.
