Eicher Motors: SEBI Takeover Disclosure Filed by Promoter-Linked Entities and Individuals

Regulatory 18 Jun 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

The disclosure filed with the National Stock Exchange (NSE) by Harish Chawla, members of the Lal family (Simran, Tara, Siddhartha), and associated trusts and entities (including Karvansarai Investments Private Limited) indicates a substantial change in share acquisition or control among existing promoter stakeholders of Eicher Motors. Under Regulation 31(4)–(5) of the SEBI Takeover Code, any individual or entity acquiring 25% or more of voting rights, or acquiring shares such that their aggregate crosses 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 25%, 30%, 40%, 50%, 60%, 70%, 80%, or 90%, must disclose to the stock exchange within 4 trading days.
