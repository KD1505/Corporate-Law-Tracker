Waaree Renewable acquires 55% controlling stake in power infrastructure firm APSPL for ₹1,225 crore via mixed primary-secondary transaction

M&A / JV 18 Jun 2026· ₹1,225 crore · Completed· ✓ Verified

WRTL acquired both newly issued equity shares and existing shares held by the Desai, Kothari, and Patel family trusts, plus founder Ajay Patel, in a two-tier structure: Share Purchase and Subscription Agreement (covering fresh issuance and secondary purchase) and Shareholders Agreement (governing inter-se director nomination, pre-emptive rights, and capital structure restrictions post-acquisition). APSPL, founded 1996, operates in power transmission tower fabrication, galvanised structures, and EPC services with ₹1,226 crore FY25 revenue and ₹339 crore net worth.
