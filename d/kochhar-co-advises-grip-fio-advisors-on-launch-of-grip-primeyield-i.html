Kochhar & Co advises Grip FIO Advisors on launch of Grip PrimeYield – I

Regulatory 14 Aug 2026 · Completed· Reported source

Grip PrimeYield – I is a Category II Alternative Investment Fund scheme established by Grip FIO Advisors LLP, the Investment Manager to the existing Grip Fixed Income Opportunities Trust (SEBI registration IN/AIF2/23-24/1284). The scheme focuses on diversified debt and debt-related instruments including non-convertible debentures, corporate bonds, structured credit instruments and securitised debt instruments, with emphasis on investment-grade opportunities and short-duration exposures. Kochhar & Co provided legal counsel on Indian regulatory aspects of fund structure and drafted all relevant documentation.

Why it mattersCategory II debt AIFs must navigate post-May 2025 SEBI amendments treating listed debt rated 'A' or below as equivalent to unlisted securities for regulatory compliance-a structuring precedent for debt-focused alternative funds in India's evolving investment landscape.
