JSA advises RAK Ceramics India on ₹200 crore NCD issuance

Banking & Finance 25 Jun 2026· ₹200 cr · Announced· ◔ Reported

R.A.K. Ceramics India Private Limited has made an issuance of senior, secured, rated, listed, redeemable, non-convertible debentures (“NCDs”) on a private placement basis, aggregating to ₹200 crore. JSA Advocates & Solicitors advised RAK Ceramics India on this NCD issuance. The Firm assisted with respect to the drafting, negotiation, and execution of the financing documents, ensuring legal and regulatory compliance.
