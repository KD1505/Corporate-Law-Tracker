BIL Vyapar Limited: 16th Committee of Creditors Meeting Outcome

IBC 6 Jul 2026 · In regulatory review· ✓ Verified

BIL Vyapar Limited, a consumer retail entity, filed for CIRP and has progressed through multiple Committee of Creditors meetings. The 16th CoC meeting outcome filing to the NSE indicates ongoing creditor engagement on restructuring or liquidation modalities. No plan approval or liquidation order is yet evident from this disclosure. The frequency and depth of CoC deliberations typically reflect complexity in asset valuation, creditor composition, or sponsor/bidder interest.

Why it mattersRepeated CoC meetings at this stage suggest either deadlock on a resolution plan, extended negotiation phases, or multiple plan submissions under Regulation 39 of IBBI; practitioners should track whether a plan has been submitted and whether the 180-day (extendable to 270-day) statutory window remains in play.
