Lenskart Solutions Limited incorporates step-down subsidiary in Republic of Korea

M&A / JV 12 Aug 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

The Board of Directors of Lenskart Solutions Limited approved incorporation of a step-down subsidiary in the Republic of Korea (company name and exact incorporation details not yet disclosed). The filing indicates this is a material corporate action requiring stock exchange intimation under Listing Rules 30. The subsidiary will enable direct market entry and localized operations in South Korea, a key Asian market for eyewear retail.

Why it mattersStep-down subsidiary structure (Indian parent → intermediate holding company → Korean operating entity) is the standard tax-efficient route for Indian tech/consumer exporters; triggers FEMA compliance, overseas investment Board reporting, and potential transfer pricing scrutiny under Rules 10AB/10AC.
