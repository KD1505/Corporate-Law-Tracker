Anand Rathi SSBL: Pradeep Navratan Gupta substantial acquisition disclosure filed under SEBI Takeover Code

Regulatory 6 Jul 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

Pradeep Navratan Gupta submitted a disclosure to NSE on 6 July 2026 under Regulation 31(4) of SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011, indicating a substantial change in shareholding or control of ARSSBL. The regulation mandates disclosure within two trading days of acquisition of ≥5% of voting capital. The filing does not yet reveal the acquisition size, mode (open market, negotiated purchase, or other), or the final consideration, only that the trigger event has occurred and been formally notified to the exchange. This is a regulatory-gating event that sets in motion review of open offer exemptions, creeping acquisition rules, and public shareholding…

Why it mattersA Reg 31(4) disclosure is the gating trigger for all downstream takeover obligations (open offer, creeping acquisition exemptions, public announcement thresholds); the filing date locks the reference date for calculating acquisition consideration and triggering entity-level structuring choices.
