Khaitan advises President of India on ₹5,549 cr Coal India share sale

IPO / ECM 6 Jun 2026· ₹5,549 cr · Completed· ◔ Reported

The President of India, acting through the Ministry of Coal, made an offer for sale of 123,279,566 equity shares of Coal India Limited through the stock-exchange mechanism, aggregating to approximately ₹5,549 crore. Khaitan & Co advised the seller. Axis Capital, ICICI Securities, JM Financial, Kotak Securities and SBICAP Securities acted as brokers to the offer.
