JNK India Limited: Monitoring Agency Report on IPO Proceeds Utilisation (Q1 FY27)

IPO / ECM 13 Aug 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

JNK India Limited filed a monitoring agency report dated 13 August 2026, certified by CRISIL Ratings Limited, covering the quarter ended 30 June 2026 (Q1 FY27). The report tracks deployment of IPO capital against the objects stated in the company's offer document. SEBI requires listed companies to appoint an independent monitoring agency and file quarterly progress reports under LODR Reg 32(6) and ICDR Reg 41, with disclosure to the stock exchange (here, NSE). This is a standard post-IPO governance checkpoint; the report itself does not disclose individual utilisation figures in the headline, but is lodged as a formal regulatory filing.

Why it mattersMandatory monitoring-agency reporting under SEBI LODR Reg 32(6) and ICDR Reg 41 is a standard gate in Indian public-company governance; the timing and completeness of utilisation disclosures can trigger investor and regulator scrutiny if material commitments slip.
