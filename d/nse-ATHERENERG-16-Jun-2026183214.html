Ather Energy Takeover Disclosure: Swapnil Jain Regulation 31(4) Filing

Regulatory 16 Jun 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

Swapnil Babanlal Jain submitted a formal disclosure to NSE under the SEBI Takeover Code, filed on 16 June 2026. The disclosure type (Reg. 31(4)) requires disclosure of substantial share purchases or concert arrangements. The specific shareholding percentage, acquisition mode (market purchase, off-market, pledge release or concert), and whether an open offer is mandated have not yet been publicly released beyond the regulatory filing.

Why it mattersRegulation 31(4) mandates public disclosure when acquisition thresholds are crossed; the filing flags a potential open offer or board seat acquisition trigger, requiring review of whether 25% threshold or board composition changes impose mandatory offer obligations.
