GACM Technologies Limited completes allotment of 495 million securities under QIP

PE / VC 17 Aug 2026 · Completed· ✓ Verified

GACM Technologies Limited held its allotment meeting on 17 August 2026 and formally allotted 495,000,000 (495 million) equity securities pursuant to a Qualified Institution Placement. The NSE Regulation 30 announcement confirms regulatory notification to the stock exchange within the prescribed timeline. The QIP route allows listed companies to raise capital from qualified institutional buyers without a public offer, subject to SEBI approval and pricing board resolutions. Post-allotment, the company must file credit confirmation and listing approval applications.

Why it mattersQIP allotments in India require strict timing compliance under SEBI's composite guidelines; this notification signals the deal has crossed the key gates (pricing lock, institutional subscription, allotment board approval) and now moves to listing/credit of securities, triggering post-allotment disclosure obligations.
