Sammaan Capital Limited: Monitoring Agency Report for Q1 FY26–27

Regulatory 14 Aug 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

Sammaan Capital appointed CRISIL Ratings Limited as monitoring agency to oversee deployment of proceeds from three concurrent capital-raising channels: rights issue, qualified institutional placement (QIP) and preferential issue. The report covers the quarter ended 30 June 2026 and is mandated under SEBI LODR Regulation 32(6), read with ICDR Regulations 82(4), 162A(4) and 173A(4). Filing confirms ongoing compliance with proceeds-utilization undertakings.

Why it mattersQuarterly monitoring certification is a regulatory gate for capital-raising proceeds utilization; delays or deviations in deployment require disclosure and may trigger compliance scrutiny under LODR.
