Polycab enters group captive arrangement with Continuum Green Energy for renewable power at Jamnagar facility

PE / VC 22 Jun 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

Polycab acquired equity in Continuum Green Energy to establish a group captive power arrangement for its Jamnagar manufacturing facility. The transaction structure involved negotiation of a Power Consumption Agreement and Share Purchase and Shareholders Agreement. This deal reflects the evolving C&I sector strategy toward renewable energy captive procurement and aligns with Electricity (Amendment) Rules 2026, which codify the 26% ownership / 51% consumption thresholds and establish a statutory two-tier captive status verification mechanism (state nodal agency for intra-state; NLDC for inter-state arrangements).
