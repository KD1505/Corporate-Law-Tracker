Crawford Bayley, Luthra and Luthra act on Master Chains & Jewels ₹400 crore+ proposed IPO

IPO / ECM 27 Aug 2026· ₹400 cr · Filed / open· ◔ Reported

Master Chains N Jewels Limited has filed its draft red herring prospectus with SEBI and the Stock Exchanges in relation to an initial public offering comprising a fresh issue of equity shares aggregating up to ₹400 crore and an offer for sale of up to 5,906,250 equity shares by the promoter selling shareholder. Crawford Bayley & Co is advising Master Chains & Jewels on this IPO. Luthra and Luthra Law Offices India are advising the Book Running Lead Manager Systematix Corporate Services Limited.
