Aye Finance posts Q4 FY2026 profit of ₹86 crores, up 110% YoY, on enlarged post-IPO networth

IPO / ECM 27 Apr 2026 · Completed· ✓ Verified

Aye Finance, a non-bank lender focused on MSMEs and underserved segments, announced strong Q4 FY2026 earnings with net profit of ₹86 crores, representing 110% year-on-year growth. The company reported a quarterly return on equity (ROE) of 16% calculated on the enlarged networth post-IPO capital infusion. The announcement was disclosed via an official press release filed with NSE on 27 April 2026, signalling robust operational scaling and RBI compliance in the regulated NBFC space.

Why it mattersPost-IPO NBFC profitability announcements benchmark market expectations for RBI-regulated financial services platforms; this ROE and growth trajectory will set precedent for peer valuations and investor appetite for regulated lending fintechs in the institutional capital market.
