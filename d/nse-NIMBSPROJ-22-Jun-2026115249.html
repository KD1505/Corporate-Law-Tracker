Brothers Trading Private Limited files SEBI Reg 31(4) disclosure for substantial acquisition in Nimbus Projects Limited

Disclosure archive · not counted as a separate transaction. Routine compliance/disclosure, not proof of a new economic transaction

M&A / JV 22 Jun 2026 · Filed / open· Official filing linked · facts not fully audited

The disclosure was submitted by Brothers Trading Private Limited to NSE under the mandatory framework of SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011. Regulation 31(4) requires that once an acquirer crosses prescribed thresholds (typically 5% voting rights or appointment to board), an open disclosure must be filed within two trading days of the trigger event. The filing is lodged in the NSE corporate archives, classifying this as a public takeover event subject to SEBI scrutiny and minority shareholder protections.

Why it mattersRegulation 31(4) disclosure signals a triggering event-acquisition of shares crossing the 5% or board-seat threshold-requiring full disclosure of acquirer identity, consideration, conditions and intent; filing marks the public announcement phase of a takeover subject to SEBI gating.
