BIL Vyapar Limited: Fifteenth Meeting of Committee of Creditors in CIRP

IBC 29 Jun 2026 · In regulatory review· ✓ Verified

BIL Vyapar Limited, an industrial entity, is undergoing Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process initiated under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016. The company has notified the National Stock Exchange of the convening of its fifteenth Committee of Creditors meeting, a procedural step required under IBC s. 27 for creditor-overseen approval of resolution plans and material decisions. The fifteenth meeting indicates this CIRP has progressed through multiple rounds of creditor deliberation, suggesting complexity in plan evaluation or asset realisation.

Why it mattersThe progression to a fifteenth CoC meeting signals extended CIRP timelines; counsel should review whether the debtor company or any resolution applicant has faced repeated extensions under IBC s. 33(4), and assess the cumulative cost and feasibility implications for stakeholder outcomes.
