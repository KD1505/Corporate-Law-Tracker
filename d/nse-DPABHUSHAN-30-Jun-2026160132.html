Vikas Kataria discloses substantial acquisition in D. P. Abhushan Limited under SEBI Takeover Code

M&A / JV 30 Jun 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

Vikas Kataria has filed a Regulation 31(4) disclosure with the National Stock Exchange concerning D. P. Abhushan Limited, a disclosure mechanism under the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011. Regulation 31(4) mandates disclosure of the nature, timing, and intent of the acquisition by the acquirer. The filing does not specify the acquisition quantum or consideration at this stage; however, such disclosures typically precede formal open-offer announcements or exemption applications under Schedule V or VI of the SAST Regulations. The timeline suggests the disclosure was made within the prescribed window following crossing of the statutory threshold.

Why it mattersRegulation 31(4) disclosure is a precursor to either a formal open offer or exemption ruling; the partner must confirm whether Kataria has triggered the 25% threshold and assess exemption eligibility (related-party, debt conversion, or inheritance routes) under SEBI SAST Regulations 2011.
