MOIL Limited Disclosure under SEBI Takeover Regulations

Regulatory 7 Jul 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

MOIL Limited, a Manganese Ore Producing Company listed on NSE and BSE and a Government of India enterprise under the Ministry of Steel, has submitted a formal disclosure to the exchange under Regulation 31(4) of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011. This disclosure is a mandatory filing when a party acquires or proposes to acquire shares or voting rights that cross specified thresholds. The disclosure does not by itself confirm a takeover bid; it indicates that shareholding changes have occurred or are contemplated and must be transparently reported to the market.

Why it mattersA Reg 31(4) disclosure is triggered by crossing thresholds (typically 25% or significant steps) in shareholding; this filing indicates either a potential acquirer has crossed a disclosure trigger or the company is responding to material change in shareholding structure - partners should monitor for follow-up open offer or exemption filings.
