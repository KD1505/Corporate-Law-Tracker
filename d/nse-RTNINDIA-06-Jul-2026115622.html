Arbutus Consultancy LLP Files SEBI Takeover Disclosure for RattanIndia Enterprises

Regulatory 30 Jun 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

Arbutus Consultancy LLP submitted a formal disclosure under Regulation 31(4) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 to the National Stock Exchange regarding RattanIndia Enterprises Limited. The timing and filing reference (team_sandeshc_30062026122300_48.pdf) indicate this was a June 2026 regulatory event. No public information on acquisition value, shareholding percentage, or open offer timeline is available from accessible sources at this time.

Why it mattersA Regulation 31(4) disclosure signals crossing of the 25% threshold or commencement of a mandatory open offer; practitioners must verify whether Arbutus is a new promoter or acting in concert, and whether the open offer is underway or pending.
