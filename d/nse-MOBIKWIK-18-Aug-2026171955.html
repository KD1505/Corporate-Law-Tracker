One Mobikwik Systems Limited: Slump Sale Completion Intimation

M&A / JV 18 Aug 2026 · Completed· ✓ Verified

One Mobikwik Systems Limited, a listed fintech company, has informed the NSE of the completion of a slump sale (asset transfer sale as a single unit without apportionment). The transaction was notified via a corporate action intimation filed with the exchange on 18 August 2026. A slump sale is a statutory mechanism (under Schedule II of the Income-Tax Act) permitting a company to sell assets en bloc; this structure avoids individual asset revaluation and simplifies transfer mechanics. The transaction has reached completion stage, though the seller, buyer, purchase price, and underlying business/asset carve-out remain non-public in the immediate announcement.

Why it mattersSlump sales of fintech/payments platforms are relatively rare structured exits in India; this precedent flags how listed tech firms execute asset carve-outs under Schedule II (no apportionment) rules, and the disclosure obligations at both NSE and income-tax levels post-completion.
