Zota Health Care acquires 20 lakh equity shares of wholly-owned subsidiary KMHP Ventures via right issue

M&A / JV 22 Jun 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

Zota Health Care Limited notified the NSE of the acquisition of 20 lakh (2 million) equity shares of KMHP Ventures Limited, its wholly-owned subsidiary, effected through a right issue subscription mechanism. The transaction structure-a parent company subscribing to a subsidiary's right issue-is typically used to inject capital into subsidiaries while maintaining voting control and avoiding dilution of third-party stakeholders. The exact consideration per share and total deal value were not disclosed in the NSE announcement. Given KMHP Ventures' wholly-owned status pre-transaction, the post-acquisition shareholding remains 100% with the parent.

Why it mattersIntra-group capitalisation via right issue requires careful drafting of subscription terms and board resolutions to ensure compliance with Companies Act gift/benefit provisions and to document arm's length pricing where subsidiary shares are non-listed.
