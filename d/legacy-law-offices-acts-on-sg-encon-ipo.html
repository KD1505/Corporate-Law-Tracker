Legacy Law Offices acts on SG Encon IPO

IPO / ECM 6 Oct 2026· ₹75 cr · Filed / open· Reported source

SG Encon Limited has filed its Draft Red Herring Prospectus before SEBI for a book-built offer on the BSE and NSE Mainboard platform for an issue of 85,61,700 shares, comprising a fresh issue and an offer for sale by Promoter Selling Shareholders Rajiv Gandhi and Pankaj Setia, with an offer size of ₹75 crore+. Legacy Law Offices advised SG Encon, the Book Running Lead Manager (Marwadi Chandarana Brokers Intermediaries Private Limited), and the Promoter Selling Shareholders (Rajiv Gandhi and Pankaj Setia) on this IPO.
