NCLT issues further directions in Concord Enviro Systems Scheme of Arrangement following shareholder approval

Regulatory 11 Jun 2026 · In regulatory review· ✓ Verified

The NCLT Mumbai Bench approved Concord Enviro's Scheme of Arrangement on March 11, 2026, with the core objective to correct the company's financial statements by offsetting its negative retained earnings using the credit balance available in its securities premium reserve. Equity shareholders approved the scheme at a court-convened general meeting on April 28, 2026, with voting results showing requisite majority. Key financial figures include secured creditor value of ₹2.97 crore, unsecured creditor value of ₹6.63 crore, and net worth post-scheme of ₹208.45 crore. The 11 June 2026 order provides further directions towards final NCLT sanction.

Why it mattersFinancial restructuring via Scheme of Arrangement under Sections 230–232 does not require merger/demerger consideration mechanics; it illustrates how listed companies use securities premium credit to cleanse negative equity and improve covenant ratios-a pure accounting-driven restructuring distinct from M&A.
