Balmer Lawrie receives BSE fine for Q1 FY27 board composition non-compliance

Regulatory 25 Aug 2026 · Completed· ✓ Verified

Balmer Lawrie & Company Limited disclosed fines imposed by BSE Limited and National Stock Exchange of India Limited under Regulation 30 of SEBI Listing Regulations. Balmer Lawrie was previously fined ₹7,50,480 by BSE Limited for Q4FY26 non-compliance due to lapses in board composition, with violations including the absence of Independent and Woman Directors and insufficient Non-Executive Directors between March 28 and March 31, 2026. The company attributed the issue to its status as a Central Public Sector Enterprise and sought a waiver from the exchange.
