NSE IPO: Which law firms are advising the stock exchange on its public listing?

IPO / ECM 26 Jun 2026· ₹1 · Filed / open· ◔ Reported

National Stock Exchange of India Limited’s proposed initial public offering has brought in Cyril Amarchand Mangaldas , Latham & Watkins LLP, Khaitan & Co , Shardul Amarchand Mangaldas & Co and Sidley Austin LLP as legal advisors. Cyril Amarchand Mangaldas is advising NSE as Indian legal counsel. Cyril Shroff , Managing Partner, and L Viswanathan , Senior Partner, provided guidance on the transaction. Cyril Shroff and L Viswanathan The CAM team comprises Yash J Ashar , Senior Partner; Manan Lahoty , Partner and Head, Capital Markets; Devaki Mankad , Partner and Regional Co-Head, Capital Markets, West; and Oishika Dasgupta , Partner.
