Juniper Hotels Limited files SEBI Regulation 31(4) Substantial Acquisition Disclosure

M&A / JV 18 Jun 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

Juniper Hotels Limited, a prominent Indian hospitality company, has submitted a Regulation 31(4) disclosure to NSE, indicating a substantial acquisition event. This regulatory form is triggered when an acquirer crosses 25% shareholding or makes a material change thereafter. The disclosure does not reveal the acquirer's identity or transaction value at the filing stage; that detail emerges in the mandatory open offer announcement. Why it mattersRegulation 31(4) disclosure is a mandatory gating event that freezes the acquirer's ability to make further purchases above the threshold until the open offer process is complete; partners must track the mandatory offer announcement timeline (within 4 working days) and the acquirer's financing/escrow arrangements.
