BCP Asia II Holdco VII triggers SEBI Reg 31(4) disclosure in Aadhar Housing Finance

M&A / JV 18 Jun 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

BCP Asia II Holdco VII Pte. Ltd., a Singapore-domiciled private equity vehicle, has filed a formal substantial acquisition disclosure under Regulation 31(4) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011. The filing indicates the acquisition has reached an advanced stage and is conditional on satisfaction of all conditions precedent. Aadhar Housing Finance Limited is a registered and systemically important housing finance company. The 25% threshold trigger mandates either a public open offer to remaining shareholders or an exemption petition to SEBI.

Why it mattersHousing finance acquisitions above 25% trigger mandatory SEBI takeover disclosure under Reg 31(4); the acquirer must now either proceed to public open offer or seek exemption from SEBI, with HFCs subject to dual NHB + SEBI regulatory scrutiny.
