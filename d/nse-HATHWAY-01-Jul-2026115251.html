Jio Content Distribution triggers substantial acquisition disclosure in Hathway Cable & Datacom under SEBI Takeover Code

M&A / JV 1 Jul 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

Jio Content Distribution Holdings Private Limited submitted a copy of its disclosure under Regulation 31(4) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 to NSE. This disclosure is required when an acquirer crosses prescribed shareholding thresholds (typically 5% and multiples thereof) in a listed company. The filing against Hathway Cable & Datacom, a prominent cable and broadband operator, signals strategic interest in the broadcast/distribution segment. The precise shareholding percentage, consideration and conditions precedent are not disclosed in the Bar & Bench excerpt; the full regulation 31(4) disclosure document would contain detailed particulars…

Why it mattersRegulation 31(4) disclosure is mandatory within two days of crossing the 5% threshold; the filing stage indicates immediate public market notification obligations and potential open offer liability if threshold breaches trigger formal takeover protocol.
