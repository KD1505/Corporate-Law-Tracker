Pace Digitek completes acquisition via newly incorporated wholly owned subsidiary

M&A / JV 21 Aug 2026 · Completed· ✓ Verified

Pace Digitek Limited, a listed entity on NSE, has informed the stock exchange of two concurrent corporate events: (1) incorporation of a wholly owned subsidiary (WOS), and (2) completion of an acquisition effected through that subsidiary. The intimation was filed on 21 August 2026. No acquisition consideration, target entity name, or operational details were disclosed in the NSE filing extract. The structure-using a special-purpose wholly owned subsidiary as the acquisition vehicle-is routine for Indian M&A but requires post-completion disclosure, NCLT approval (if applicable), and updating of shareholding patterns.

Why it mattersThe use of a special-purpose wholly owned subsidiary as an acquisition vehicle is a standard Indian M&A structure for ring-fencing liabilities and maintaining regulatory clarity; the completion intimation triggers post-deal disclosure obligations under Listing Rules.
