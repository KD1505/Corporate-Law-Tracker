Torrent Pharmaceuticals completes amalgamation of J. B. Chemicals & Pharmaceuticals; allots 4.19 crore shares

M&A / JV 17 Jul 2026 · Completed· ✓ Verified

On 17 July 2026 (record date), Torrent Pharmaceuticals allotted 4,19,22,416 fully paid-up equity shares to eligible shareholders of J. B. Chemicals & Pharmaceuticals Limited pursuant to a Scheme of Amalgamation. The allotment, intimated to NSE on 20 July 2026, represents the completion of the scheme's share-based consideration structure. This merger combines two significant players in the Indian pharmaceutical sector and is governed by the amalgamation scheme as approved by the NCLT and sanctioned under the Companies Act, 2013. The transaction reflects the consolidation trend in mid-cap pharma.

Why it mattersPost-allotment, practitioners must ensure shareholder certification, creditor claims closure, and compliance with NCLT direction on scheme surplus or adjustment mechanisms-precedent on large pharma consolidation under current M&A regulatory architecture.
