Lakshmi Niwas Bangur files SEBI Reg 31(4) substantial acquisition disclosure in Peria Karamalai Tea

M&A / JV 1 Jul 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

Lakshmi Niwas Bangur submitted a formal disclosure to the NSE under Regulation 31(4) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011, in respect of The Peria Karamalai Tea & Produce Company Limited. The disclosure mechanism requires persons acquiring 5% or more of voting capital, or subsequent acquisitions of 2% or more (if already holding 5%+), to file within two trading days. The identity of Bangur as a natural person and the choice of NSE as the filing exchange suggest the target is NSE-listed.
