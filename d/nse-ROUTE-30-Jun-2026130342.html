Route Mobile Limited: Substantial Acquisition of Shares Disclosure under SEBI Takeover Regulations

M&A / JV 30 Jun 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

Route Mobile Limited, a Mumbai-based technology and telecom solutions provider, has submitted a mandatory disclosure document to NSE under Regulation 31(4) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011. This regulation mandates that any person acquiring or proposing to acquire shares and voting rights in a listed company such that the aggregate holding reaches or exceeds 25% of the company's capital must disclose this intent to the exchange and the company. The filing was submitted to NSE's corporate disclosure portal on or around 30 June 2026.

Why it mattersA Regulation 31(4) disclosure triggers once an acquirer crosses or announces intention to cross 25% threshold; the filing indicates either imminent or completed substantial share acquisition requiring immediate market transparency and potential open offer obligations.
