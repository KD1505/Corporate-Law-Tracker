MRF Limited: Disclosure under SEBI Takeover Regulations

Regulatory 19 Jun 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

K M Mammen submitted a Regulation 31(4) disclosure to the NSE under SEBI's Takeover Code. This regulation mandates disclosure when a person (or group) crosses key acquisition thresholds or assumes control of a target company. The filing was made via NSE's corporate announcements portal on 19 June 2026. The specific shareholding quantity, acquisition timeline, and consideration remain to be corroborated from the filed disclosure document or MRF's investor relations announcements.

Why it mattersReg 31(4) disclosures flag changes in control thresholds (typically 25%–26% acquisition or board composition shift); partners must assess whether a full takeover notice or creeping acquisition exemption applies, and confirm no regulatory breach of the 90-day disclosure window.
