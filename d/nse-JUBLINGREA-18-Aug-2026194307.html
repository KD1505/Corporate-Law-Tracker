Jubilant Ingrevia to acquire 40% strategic equity stake in Zettaone Technologies via binding term sheet

M&A / JV 18 Aug 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

Jubilant Ingrevia Limited notified the National Stock Exchange of a board resolution approving entry into a binding term sheet with Zettaone Technologies India Private Limited for acquisition of a 40% strategic equity stake. The transaction represents a strategic move into the technology sector. Consideration value, transaction timeline, and closing conditions have not been disclosed in the initial exchange intimation. Subsequent filings will clarify the deal structure, investment amount, and regulatory approvals required.

Why it mattersBinding term sheet (not definitive agreement) signals deal intent but leaves structural/valuation terms and regulatory approvals (FIPB, CCI, sectoral caps if applicable) still to be finalized; monitor for definitive agreement announcement and competition law filing requirements.
