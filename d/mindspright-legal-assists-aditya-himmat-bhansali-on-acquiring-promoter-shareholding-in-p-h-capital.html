Mindspright Legal assists Aditya Himmat Bhansali on acquiring promoter shareholding in PH Capital

Regulatory 25 Jun 2026· ₹45,07,66,792 · Completed· ◔ Reported

Aditya Himmat Bhansali has acquired the promoter shareholding in PH Capital Limited from Sejal Rikeen Dalal, Tej Pradip Dalal, Sujalbhai Vikrambhai Palkhiwala, Rikeen P. Dalal HUF, Krishna Trust, Tirupati Trust, and Ruby Multimedia Private Limited, for ₹45,07,66,792/-. Mindspright Legal advised and represented the Aditya Himmat Bhansali on this acquisition.
