Reliance Home Finance Limited: 11th CoC Meeting Rescheduled to 24 June 2026

IBC 18 Jun 2026 · In regulatory review· ✓ Verified

Reliance Home Finance Limited, listed on NSE, informed the exchange on 18 June 2026 that the 11th meeting of its Committee of Creditors-scheduled for 15 June 2026 at 3:00 PM IST-had been rescheduled to 24 June 2026 at 11:00 AM IST, to be held via video conferencing. The disclosure was made under SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, Regulation 30, and Schedule III, Para A, sub-clause 16(g), mandating listed companies to promptly announce material developments in insolvency proceedings. No reason for the postponement was stated in the filing. The company entered CIRP following creditor action under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016.

Why it mattersProcedural delays in CoC meetings during CIRP are common but signal that resolution negotiations remain fluid; creditor alignment on a resolution plan or liquidation recommendation is not yet locked, and the 120-day statutory window (extendable to 180 days under IBC §12(3)) remains critical for filing a plan.
