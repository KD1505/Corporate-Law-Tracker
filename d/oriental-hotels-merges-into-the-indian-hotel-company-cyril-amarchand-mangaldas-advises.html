<cite index="3-7,3-10">IHCL approves merger of associate Oriental Hotels via all-stock scheme at 25:117 share ratio, targeting second-half FY2028 completion with appointed date of April 1, 2027</cite>

M&A / JV 24 Aug 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

Oriental Hotels is an associate company of IHCL with a portfolio of seven hotels and 825 rooms, including freehold assets Taj Coromandel–Chennai, Taj Fisherman's Cove Resort & Spa–Chennai, Gateway Coonoor, and leasehold assets Taj Malabar Resort & Spa–Cochin, Vivanta Coimbatore, Vivanta Mangalore, Gateway Madurai. IHCL held 37.05% of OHL's equity as of June 30, 2026. The scheme is subject to statutory approvals and clearances and proposes simplifying the group's holding structure by increasing IHCL's direct ownership, resulting in two new operating subsidiaries.

Why it mattersScheme of Arrangement under Sections 230–232 of Companies Act 2013 requires NCLT sanction plus shareholder, creditor, stock exchange and SEBI approvals-a multi-gated restructuring requiring careful statutory sequencing and timing management.
