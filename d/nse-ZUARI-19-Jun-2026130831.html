Jyotsna Poddar files SEBI Takeover disclosure under Regulation 31(4)

M&A / JV 19 Jun 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

The filing is a Regulation 31(4) disclosure under SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011, submitted to NSE by or on behalf of Jyotsna Poddar. This regulation requires disclosure when acquisition thresholds (typically 25% of voting capital or creeping acquisition of 2% in a financial year) are reached or exceeded. The specific stake percentage, acquisition price, and offeror identity must be verified from the original filing document.

Why it mattersThe disclosure triggers mandatory reporting of open offers, creeping acquisition thresholds, or exempted acquisitions; counsel must verify whether a formal open offer is required and whether PSU/related-party exemptions apply.
