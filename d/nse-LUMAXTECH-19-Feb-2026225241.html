Lumax Auto Technologies: GST Demand Reduced in State Tax Appeal

Regulatory 18 Feb 2026· ₹1,03,78,580 · Ruling· ✓ Verified

Lumax Auto Technologies Limited, a listed automotive components manufacturer, received an Order-in-Appeal dated 18 February 2026 from the Joint Commissioner of State Tax, Appeals, Pune Division-2 (Maharashtra). The first appeal against an initial GST demand has been partly allowed. The original demand, including interest and penalty, stood at ₹3,52,19,923; the appellate order has reduced this to ₹1,03,78,580-a reduction of approximately ₹2.48 crore. The company intimated this ruling to the National Stock Exchange under Regulation 30 of the Listing Regulations on 19 February 2026.

Why it mattersPartial success in tax appeal demonstrates risk mitigation on significant indirect tax exposure; the 70% reduction signals potential weakness in the original assessment and may support precedent for similar GST treatment in the automotive component sector.
