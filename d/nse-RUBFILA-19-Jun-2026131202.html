Minal Bharat Patel files substantial acquisition disclosure under SEBI Takeover Code

M&A / JV 19 Jun 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

Minal Bharat Patel's disclosure under Regulation 31(4) of the SEBI Takeover Regulations indicates a substantial acquisition event in Rubfila International Limited and has been officially filed with the NSE. Regulation 31(4) mandates disclosure when a person acquires shares or voting rights and such acquisition requires determination of whether an open offer obligation is triggered or an exemption applies. The disclosure filing with the exchange marks the formal commencement of the statutory review period under the Takeover Code. The acquirer's exact shareholding, acquisition route, and exemption claim (if any) are typically detailed in the Form G filing appended to the disclosure.

Why it mattersRegulation 31(4) disclosures initiate the statutory timeline for either an open offer (if triggering the 25% threshold) or a binding exemption opinion from the SEBI takeover panel; counsel must map the acquirer's intention (strategic, financial, creeping acquisition) and confirm whether exemption eligibility has been pre-vetted.
