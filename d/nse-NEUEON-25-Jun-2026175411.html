Neueon Corporation Limited: NCLT approves reclassification of erstwhile promoter(s) under approved resolution plan

IBC 23 Oct 2024 · Completed· ✓ Verified

Neueon Corporation Limited, a company undergoing insolvency resolution, obtained NCLT approval on 23 October 2024 for reclassification of erstwhile promoter(s) under an approved resolution plan. The delay in NSE disclosure (intimation filed 25 June 2026) suggests either a recent trigger event or procedural compliance filing. Under SEBI Regulation 31A, any change in promoter classification requires immediate intimation to the exchange and disclosure of the new promoter's identity, shareholding pattern, and source of funds, subject to the resolution plan's commercial terms and NCLT order directions.

Why it mattersPromoter reclassification under IBC resolution plans triggers mandatory SEBI Listing Regulations disclosure; counsel must verify NCLT order recitals on grounds for reclassification and confirm new promoter eligibility criteria before public announcement to avoid Regulation 31A breach.
