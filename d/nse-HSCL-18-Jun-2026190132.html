Himadri Speciality Chemical Limited announces acquisition transaction

M&A / JV 18 Jun 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

Himadri Speciality Chemical Limited, a speciality chemicals manufacturer listed on NSE, intimated an acquisition to the exchange on 18 June 2026. Public press releases, BSE/NSE detailed announcements, and business media have not yet published the transaction terms.
