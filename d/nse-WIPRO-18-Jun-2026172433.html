Wipro to acquire additional 20% stake in Aggne Global entities

M&A / JV 18 Jun 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

Wipro Limited, via step-down subsidiary Wipro IT Services LLC, previously acquired 60% of Aggne Global IT Services Private Limited (India entity) and Aggne Global Inc. (US entity) on 14 February 2024. The current announcement (18 June 2026) discloses acquisition of an additional 20%, bringing aggregate holding to 80%, with expected completion by 30 June 2026. The NSE Regulation 30 intimation does not disclose consideration, deal structure, remaining shareholder rights, or any minority put/call mechanisms. The transaction targets a technology services group with India and US operating entities, consistent with Wipro's inorganic M&A strategy in IT services.

Why it mattersStaged acquisition structures in cross-border IT services deals trigger Reg 30 disclosure duties at each material step; partners must confirm whether remaining 20% minority consent or call provisions exist, and whether any earn-out or deferred consideration is embedded in the seller's non-disclosure.
