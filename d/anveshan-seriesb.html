CMS INDUSLAW advises Anveshan on ₹150 cr Series B

PE / VC 6 Jun 2026· ₹150 cr · Announced· ◔ Reported

Anveshan Farm Technologies raised ₹150 crore in a Series B led by Vertex Ventures and IFC Co-Invest, with participation from Swiggy co-founder Sriharsha Majety and existing investors. CMS INDUSLAW advised Anveshan, with tax support. Anveshan makes clean, traceable foods including A2 ghee, cold-pressed oils and honey.
