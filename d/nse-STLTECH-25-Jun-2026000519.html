Sterlite Technologies Limited: Qualified Institutions Placement (QIP) of Equity Shares

IPO / ECM 24 Jun 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

Sterlite Technologies, a listed telecom-infrastructure and speciality optical-fibre player, initiated a QIP under the SEBI ICDR Regulations 2018 Chapter VI framework and Companies Act 2013 Section 42. QIPs permit listed companies to issue fresh equity to 'qualified institutional buyers' (mutual funds, insurance companies, provident funds, foreign portfolio investors, banks) without prior shareholder approval, subject to board/audit committee sign-off and pricing at a floor determined by a regulatory formula. The exchange intimation, dated 24 Jun 2026, signalled the placement opening; the specific quantum and issue price were not disclosed in this intimation.

Why it mattersQIPs under Chapter VI ICDR are 'deemed approval' issuances (no shareholder vote, pricing at floor + 5% discount discretion), requiring only board/audit committee clearance-the structuring choice avoids a general meeting and enables faster capital raise for listed technology firms.
