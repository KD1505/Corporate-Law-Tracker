Lenskart Solutions Limited announces incorporation of step-down subsidiary in China

M&A / JV 17 Aug 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

Lenskart Solutions Limited, a listed eyewear and optical retail company, notified the NSE of the incorporation of Wenzhou Framekart Trade Co., Ltd in the People's Republic of China. The company is classified as a step-down subsidiary, meaning it is held indirectly through another Lenskart subsidiary rather than directly by the parent. This structure typically supports international supply-chain integration or market-specific operations. No acquisition price, equity stake percentage, or expected timeline for operational launch was disclosed in the exchange intimation. The filing was made under the Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements (LODR), 2015, Regulation 30 (material events).

Why it mattersThe step-down subsidiary structure (subsidiary-of-subsidiary) signals staged international expansion into eyewear trade in China's Wenzhou manufacturing hub, with potential structuring implications under FEMA Schedule-1 (overseas direct investment) and Indian tax residence treaty planning.
