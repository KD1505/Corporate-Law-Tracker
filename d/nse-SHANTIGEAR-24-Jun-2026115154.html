Tube Investments of India discloses substantial acquisition in Shanthi Gears under SEBI Takeover Regulations

M&A / JV 24 Jun 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

Tube Investments of India Limited, a diversified engineering and manufacturing conglomerate, has submitted a formal disclosure to the NSE under Regulation 31(4) and 31(5) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011. This disclosure is a prerequisite step in the takeover process and signals either the crossing of a substantial shareholding threshold (typically 25%) or the commencement of a mandatory open offer. The exact shareholding position, consideration, and whether a full open offer will follow have not been disclosed in the available public summary.
