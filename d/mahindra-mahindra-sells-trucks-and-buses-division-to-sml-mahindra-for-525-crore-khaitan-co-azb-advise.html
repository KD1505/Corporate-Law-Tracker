<b>Mahindra & Mahindra transfers Trucks & Buses Division to listed subsidiary SML Mahindra</b> in a ₹525 crore slump sale, consolidating group's >3.5T commercial vehicle business

M&A / JV 29 Jul 2026· ₹525 cr · Announced· ✓ Verified

M&M's Truck & Bus Division generated ₹2,989 crore in revenue (FY26), representing ~2% of M&M's total operational income and ₹481 crore of net worth (0.65%). The division sold 14,832 vehicles in FY26. Transfer includes employees, assets, IP, licences, permits, insurance, contracts, rights and liabilities as a going concern. Business Transfer Agreement to be executed on or before 7 Aug 2026; transaction completion by 31 Jan 2027 subject to shareholder and regulatory approvals. M&M will continue manufacturing Mahindra-branded vehicles under a contract manufacturing arrangement post-transfer. Deal classified as related-party transaction at arm's length; valuation independently assessed by GT…

Why it mattersSlump sale of a division that is not separately undertaking-classified (per SEBI LODR Regulation 37A inapplicability) structured via Business Transfer Agreement rather than Scheme of Arrangement—permitting faster execution while triggering related-party disclosure and arm's-length valuation requirements; contract manufacturing continuity arrangement preserves operational leverage post-transfer.
