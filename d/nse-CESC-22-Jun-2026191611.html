CESC Limited: Disclosure under SEBI Takeover Regulations by Rainbow Investments Limited

M&A / JV 22 Jun 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

Rainbow Investments Limited has submitted a formal disclosure to NSE under Regulation 31(4) of the SEBI Takeover Regulations 2011, concerning a substantial acquisition in CESC Limited (a Kolkata-based integrated power utility). The disclosure triggers mandatory public announcement and transparency requirements. The actual shareholding percentage, consideration amount, and offer terms are documented in the filed disclosure form, which is archived in NSE's corporate action portal. This is a regulated capital markets event requiring clearance-of-title verification and minority shareholder protection mechanisms under Indian law.

Why it mattersRegulation 31(4) disclosure is a precursor to either a public open offer or a negotiated settlement; the acquirer's next step will depend on the quantum of shares acquired and whether 25% or 50%+ threshold has been crossed, determining open offer vs. exemption eligibility under the Takeover Code.
