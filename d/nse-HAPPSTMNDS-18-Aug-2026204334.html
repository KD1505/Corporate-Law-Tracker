Happiest Minds Technologies Limited files Scheme of Arrangement

M&A / JV 18 Aug 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

Happiest Minds Technologies Limited, an IT services and product engineering company, has informed the NSE of a Scheme of Arrangement effective 18 August 2026 under Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements. The scheme involves a corporate reorganisation but specific details regarding merging parties, consideration, conditions precedent and NCLT approval status require review of the full scheme document and NCLT order.

Why it mattersScheme of Arrangement filings under Regulation 30 signal a material corporate event requiring NCLT approval and shareholder consent; counsel must verify the scheme's statutory classification (merger, amalgamation, or restructuring), identify the merging entities, and confirm compliance with Companies Act 2013 procedures and SEBI takeover/delisting rules if applicable.
