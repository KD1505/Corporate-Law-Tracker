QSR Management Trust files Regulation 31(4) disclosure under SEBI Takeover Code for Sapphire Foods

M&A / JV 19 Jun 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

QSR Management Trust has submitted a disclosure under Regulation 31(4) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 to the National Stock Exchange. This disclosure mechanism is triggered when a person or group (including concert parties) acquires shares crossing specified thresholds (typically 5% incremental for listed entities). Sapphire Foods India Limited operates QSR brands including Hari Om Restaurants and other food & beverage franchises. The filing indicates either a fresh substantial acquisition or movement across a regulatory threshold necessitating public disclosure and potential open-offer obligations.

Why it mattersA Regulation 31(4) disclosure (vs 31(1)) indicates acquisition of shares that, together with concert party holdings, cross a regulatory threshold; counsel must track the concert-party scope, voting agreements and any creeping-acquisition exemptions under Regulation 10(1), and monitor for a mandatory open offer obligation under Regulation 3 if thresholds are breached.
