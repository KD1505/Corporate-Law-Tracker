Dr. Nikhil Kelkar acquires 16,400 equity shares in Hexagon Nutrition Limited

Regulatory 19 Aug 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

Dr. Nikhil Kelkar, holding the dual role of Promoter and Joint Managing Director at Hexagon Nutrition Limited, acquired 16,400 equity shares. The transaction was disclosed to NSE via formal intimation on 19 August 2026 under SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011, Regulation 29(2), which mandates disclosure of acquisitions that cross statutory thresholds or are material to the company. No acquisition price or total consideration was disclosed in the filing.

Why it mattersPromoter share acquisitions by board-level insiders require real-time Regulation 29(2) disclosure to the exchange and trigger insider trading window compliance reviews; the materiality threshold and band-crossing mechanics define when such acquisitions become public knowledge.
