Simbhaoli Sugars Limited enters Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process; first CoC meeting scheduled 23 July 2026

IBC 21 Jul 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

Simbhaoli Sugars Limited, a publicly listed sugar manufacturer based in Uttar Pradesh, has entered CIRP. The company formally intimated the NSE of the first CoC meeting scheduled for Thursday, 23 July 2026, triggering mandatory disclosure obligations. The CoC will constitute the creditor body responsible for approving the Insolvency Professional's preliminary report, evaluating resolution plans, and determining the path to either resolution or liquidation under the IBC regime.

Why it mattersSugar sector CIRP filings remain subject to sector-specific moratoriums under the Sugarcane Control Order; resolution plan design must address farmer dues (priority claim under IBC s.53) and inter-creditor equity.
