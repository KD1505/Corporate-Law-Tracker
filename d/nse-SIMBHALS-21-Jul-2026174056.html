Simbhaoli Sugars Limited: Committee of Creditors Constituted in CIRP

IBC 21 Jul 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

Simbhaoli Sugars Limited, a sugar and agro-allied company with BSE/NSE listings, has formally constituted its Committee of Creditors following admission of a corporate insolvency petition. The CoC comprises representatives of all categories of creditors (financial creditors, operational creditors, and government dues holders where applicable) and is mandated to approve the appointment of an Insolvency Resolution Professional (IRP), oversee claims adjudication, and approve any Resolution Plan. This milestone indicates the company has cleared the threshold eligibility checks under IBC section 4 and Schedule II, and the CoC is now empowered to negotiate, evaluate and vote on Resolution Plans…

Why it mattersCoC constitution marks the formal commencement of the resolution window; all creditor claims and voting rights now vest in the CoC, triggering the 180-day (extendable to 330-day) resolution timeline and the suspension of individual recovery and creditor litigation rights.
