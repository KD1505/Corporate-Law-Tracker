Aegis Logistics closes ₹3,500-crore QIP; 28.93 million shares allocated at ₹1,210

IPO / ECM 16 Nov 2026· ₹3,500 cr · Completed· Document-reviewed case study

Aegis Logistics's NSE disclosure confirms that its institutional placement opened on 11 November 2026 and was closed on 16 November after receipt of applications and escrow funds from eligible QIBs. Its fundraising committee approved allocation of 28,925,619 shares of face value ₹1 at ₹1,210 per share, including ₹1,209 share premium, an implied aggregate of approximately ₹3,500 crore. The 16 November placement document and confirmation-of-allocation notice were also approved. This original document records closure and allocation; a separate final allotment filing should be checked before describing the actual legal allotment as already completed.
