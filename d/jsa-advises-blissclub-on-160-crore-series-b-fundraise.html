<b>JSA advises BlissClub on ₹160 crore Series B fundraise led by Singularity AMC</b>

PE / VC 7 Aug 2026· ₹160 cr · Completed· Reported source

BlissClub closed the ₹160 crore ($16.8 million) Series B funding round on August 7, 2026, led by Singularity AMC, with existing investors Elevation Capital and Eight Roads Ventures doubling their positions. The round drew significant personal capital commitment from founder Minu Margeret and her partner Vidit Aatrey, co-founder and CEO of social commerce platform Meesho. In fiscal year ended March 2025, BlissClub's operating revenue rose 51% to ₹131.5 crore from ₹87 crore the year before, while total losses fell 54.5% to ₹20 crore from ₹44 crore, strengthening the company's case for an expensive retail expansion bet.

Why it mattersFirst time a new institutional investor entered BlissClub's cap table since 2022, with both existing backers Elevation Capital and Eight Roads Ventures choosing to return rather than exit, signalling strong founder-investor alignment in growth-stage consumer brands.
