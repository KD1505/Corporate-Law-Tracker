Sanathan Textiles Limited Disclosure under SEBI Takeover Regulations

M&A / JV 19 Jun 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

The filing represents a structured disclosure under the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011, indicating the target has received notice of a substantial shareholding acquisition crossing mandatory thresholds. Regulation 31(4) requires the target company to immediately notify the exchange and publish details of the acquirer's identity, shareholding pattern, and acquisition structure. The disclosure obligation arises where an acquirer crosses 25% or enters into negotiations or agreements for control.

Why it mattersRegulation 31(4) disclosure triggers public acquirer identification and ongoing shareholding transparency; confirms transaction crossed the 25% threshold requiring regulatory filing within statutory timelines.
