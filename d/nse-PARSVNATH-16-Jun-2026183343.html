Edelweiss EARC discloses substantial shareholding in Parsvnath Developers under SEBI takeover regulations

M&A / JV 6 May 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

Edelweiss Asset Reconstruction Company Ltd, a special-purpose vehicle for distressed asset recovery (typically set up by banks/SEBI), made a Regulation 29(2) disclosure to NSE regarding Parsvnath Developers Limited, a publicly-listed real estate company. The disclosure mechanism under SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeover) Regulations 2011 is triggered when an acquirer crosses specified thresholds of shares or voting rights. The filing initiates public transparency and triggers mandatory offer requirements unless exemptions apply (e.g., open offer exemption for reconstruction of financially distressed companies).

Why it mattersEARC's filing signals either acquisition of >25% stake or acquisition of board control; the acquirer must comply with open offer norms (Regulation 23 SAST) and appointment/disclosure of shareholding pattern per listing rules — structurally distinct from negotiated acquisition of entire company.
