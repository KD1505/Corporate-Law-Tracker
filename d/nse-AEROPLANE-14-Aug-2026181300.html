Amir Chand Jagdish Kumar (Exports) Limited: Q1 FY26-27 Monitoring Agency Report

Regulatory 14 Aug 2026 · In force· ✓ Verified

Amir Chand Jagdish Kumar (Exports) Limited, a listed entity on NSE, appointed Care Ratings Limited as Monitoring Agency to oversee utilisation of funds raised through its public issue. The Q1 FY26-27 monitoring report was filed on 14 Aug 2026 in compliance with regulation 32(6) of the SEBI Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements (LODR) Regulations, 2015 and regulation 41(4) of the SEBI Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements (ICDR) Regulations, 2018. The report is a mandatory quarterly submission tracking deployment of capital raised and certifying adherence to the stated objects of the issue.

Why it mattersRoutine quarterly monitoring of public issue proceeds utilisation is a mandatory disclosure gating item for listed companies post-IPO; this report documents compliance with capital deployment conditions precedent to the public offering.
