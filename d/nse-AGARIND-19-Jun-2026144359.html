<b>Lalit Agarwal files annual Regulation 31(4) disclosure confirming no promoter share encumbrance in Agarwal Industrial Corporation for FY26</b>

Regulatory 3 Apr 2026 · Filed / open· ✓ Verified

The disclosure confirms that all direct and indirect shareholdings of Lalit Agarwal and the promoter group remained free of pledges or encumbrances throughout FY26. Promoter holding stood at approximately 57.2% as of the year-end. The filing was formally submitted to both BSE Limited and National Stock Exchange of India Limited via digital signature. This routine annual certification is distinct from the substantive acquisition filings under Regulation 30 and is a hygiene compliance marker.

Why it mattersAnnual Regulation 31(4) encumbrance declarations, though procedurally ministerial, serve as a key governance signal-the clean holding confirms no collateral pressure and supports credit rating stability for the bitumen-logistics business.
