Aegis Logistics in talks to acquire UAE's Tristar for $1.5 billion - NSE seeks clarification

M&A / JV 18 Aug 2026· $1.5 bn · Rumoured· ✓ Verified

The NSE requested news verification from Aegis Logistics regarding reports it was in talks to acquire Tristar Group for $1.5 billion. Tristar is a Dubai-headquartered, fully-integrated energy logistics provider owned by Kuwait's Agility Global. No response, confirmation or denial has been disclosed publicly. The transaction remains unconfirmed and the only source is the NSE clarification request itself. Agility had previously announced in September 2024 it was considering stake sales in Tristar to unlock value for shareholders.

Why it mattersCross-border energy logistics consolidation-if confirmed, would mark Aegis's first major international platform acquisition, expanding beyond India's coast-based terminal network into Middle East fuel storage and distribution.
