Akshay Poddar files substantial acquisition disclosure in Texmaco Infrastructure under SEBI Takeover Regulations

M&A / JV 19 Jun 2026 · Announced· ✓ Verified

Akshay Poddar has formally notified the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) of a substantial acquisition triggering disclosure requirements under Regulation 31(4) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011. The filing does not disclose the acquisition price, quantum of shares acquired, or the final consideration; Texmaco Infrastructure is a publicly listed company in the rail and infrastructure engineering sector. The disclosure mechanism is mandatory within 2 trading days of crossing the threshold or entering into a binding agreement.

Why it mattersA Reg 31(4) disclosure indicates crossing of a 5% shareholding threshold or a material acquisition event; this precedent underscores the strict timeline and disclosure obligations for acquirers in substantial transactions, with regulatory filings preceding or coinciding with market announcements.
