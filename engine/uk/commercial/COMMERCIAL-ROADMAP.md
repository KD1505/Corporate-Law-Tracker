# Commercial readiness programme — release 01

Owner: founder; accountable human reviewers to be identified. Start: 25 November 2026. Quality over a fixed timetable. No score, launch approval, professional review or customer traction is inferred from automated checks.

Initial paid segment: UK aspiring commercial lawyers, international graduates and entry-level legal professionals. Keep India functional and independently quality-gated. Free primary-source links and core summaries remain accessible. No payment collection, paid service provisioning, legal claims or independent endorsement until separately authorised and verified.

Counts are ambitions, not publication quotas. Public information is not automatically reusable commercially. Keep discovery separate from editorial records. Never infer sponsorship from a licence, availability from retrieval, independent review from source count or learning outcomes from completions. Existing RESEARCH-EXCELLENCE.md and careers/ROADMAP.md remain governing detail. No bypassing failing checks or unreviewed data publication. Every tranche: bounded branch, actual tests, preview, review, merge, confirm production commit, record blockers.
