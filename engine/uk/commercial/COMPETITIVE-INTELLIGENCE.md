## Benchmark protocol

Weekly rotating public-source review: log dated URL, observed change, customer problem, applicability UK/India, proposed original implementation, priority, acceptance test, dependency and release evidence. Distinguish provider claims, directly observed interfaces and actual measured outcomes. Do not mark absent/paid/untested as deficient. No user testing or subscriptions without approval.

Use a fixed comparable task set: (1) find five roles matching graduate eligibility, current deadlines and sponsorship scope; (2) find latest verified market developments over 7 days; (3) identify who advised whom and value basis with source; (4) compare two employers with dated eligibility/pay evidence; (5) prepare a transaction-linked interview answer; (6) complete a practical exercise with rubric. Record date, exact task, source availability, completion time, accuracy, errors, accessibility and limitations. No superiority claim until measured. P0 transaction freshness, not a feature-race.

Tranche 12 implements evidence-linked firm comparison with direct salary sources, programme-specific sponsorship, sourced recruitment facts, mandate dossier links and explicit unknowns. Browser fixtures test evidence links, limited scope and unavailable compensation. No new employer facts, survey claims or independent review. No paid access or data collection. Other features remain roadmap items.
