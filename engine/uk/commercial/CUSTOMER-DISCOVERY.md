# Customer discovery protocol — actual participants only

Recruit 10 UK undergraduate law students, 10 international graduates, 10 paralegal/training-contract applicants, 10 trainees/junior associates, and 5–10 careers advisers/practitioners. Obtain informed consent before recording; participation voluntary. Do not ask for visa details beyond what participants choose to share, CVs, confidential employer assessments, client documents or privileged material. Store consent and identifiable notes securely outside this public repo; publish aggregate anonymous findings only.

## 30-minute interview
1. Describe the last real job search or commercial research task. What triggered it? What did you use? How long did it take? What was difficult?
2. Show the last ordinary workflow (no private data or employer material). Observe rather than coach.
3. Using the free portal: find five suitable roles (or explain why fewer are available); explain one deal and identify original evidence; prepare three firm-specific interview points; find an approaching deadline. Record time, task completion, errors, confidence and need to use another source. Use equivalent tasks with usual tools and counterbalance order where possible.
4. Ask what would make the participant return next week, and what they would replace. Ask for specific missing features and credibility concerns.
5. Show hypothetical memberships at £6.99, £9.99, £14.99 and £19.99 in varied order. Ask which, if any, they would purchase and why. Do not take payment or count intent as sales.

## Anonymous results schema
Participant code; cohort; date; recruitment channel; task ID; tool order; duration seconds; completed yes/no/partial; factual error count; source verification success; observed blocker; stated recurring problem; proposed price choice; opt-in for follow-up stored separately and securely. Aggregate only; no personal identifiers in GitHub. Report denominator, missing data and selection bias. Do not fabricate responses.

## Decision rule
Rank problems by observed frequency, severity and repeat use; prefer improvements that measurably reduce time without reducing accuracy. Re-test after changes. Do not certify willingness to pay without real compliant paid pilot and renewal evidence.
