# Nightly research operations

UK schedule: 21:40 UTC (03:10 IST next day). India workflow unchanged. Discovery runs only on schedule or manual dispatch, not push, preventing recursive discovery snapshots. The nightly job checks the curated structural floor, collects source metadata, creates a persistent queue for new cases, changed documents, issuer filings and counsel leads, and ranks missing research, live deadlines and claim review. It records feed failures/empty responses and preserves rolled-off pending leads. The evidence pointer ledger is not an atomic claim audit and origin groups remain null until an editor establishes them. The job never auto-promotes candidates or changes uk/data.json. A new record must have substantive research fields, multiple source links, precise finding locators, typed value or documented unknown, mandate or documented gap, and workstreams. Corrective reductions require a reviewed floor amendment. If editorial data changes during collection, skip stale snapshot rather than overwriting it. HPE, Adobe, Broadcom and Figma feeds configured, not claimed operational before successful retrieval. Run node engine/uk/nightly-research.mjs --check and node --test engine/uk/nightly-research.test.mjs. All claims still require editorial document-level review; no commercial certification implied.
