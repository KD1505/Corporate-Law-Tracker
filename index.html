Methodology & verification standards

How items reach this tracker, and exactly what each label asserts.
What CorpLawTracker is

A decision engine, not a clippings file. Everything here is machine-collected nightly, verified against primary filings where they exist, and labelled honestly when they don't - then turned into the pitch to make, the precedent to cite, and the documents to re-paper.

Pipeline & cadence

An automated pipeline ingests India's corporate-law trade press and regulator feeds nightly, deduplicates against everything already tracked, structures each item (parties, value, counsel, stage), and links it to the statutes and regulations it engages. The "Updated" date in the header is the date of the last successful run - there is no intraday updating.

✓ Verified

The matter is corroborated by a specific official source - the named company's BSE/NSE filings page, a regulator's order or circular, or a court record - or has been editor-confirmed against such a filing. A generic regulator portal is never treated as verification.

Reported

Sourced to credible trade press (e.g. Bar & Bench Dealstreet) but a specific primary filing has not yet been matched. Treat as press-level reliability and open the source before relying on it.

Official tag

A source chip is marked OFFICIAL only when its URL resolves to a government, regulator, exchange or court domain (SEBI, RBI, CCI, IBBI, MCA, BSE/NSE, NCLT, e-Courts, PIB and equivalents).

Priority scoring

Items are scored on deal value, marquee parties/counsel, regulatory significance and the presence of official corroboration. The highest band is flagged PRIORITY on cards and surfaced in "What matters now".

Limits

This is a research and current-awareness tool, not legal advice. Headlines and snippets are shown under fair-use linking. Always read the primary filing and the current text of the law before advising.
